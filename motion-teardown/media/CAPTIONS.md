## f08 — Fidelity

**Caption:** Across 101 original/rebuild pairs from y = 0 to 9,000 px, overall structure SSIM is 0.782 and luminance SSIM is 0.703. The corrected hero mean is 0.366 structure / 0.382 luminance (up from 0.341 / 0.356), but remains the weakest in-scope section because the desk composition, typeface and copy positions differ. Sobel-gradient SSIM is the primary structure score; section means keep that gap visible instead of hiding it in one average.

**Alt:** A two-line chart of structure and luminance similarity over 101 scroll positions, with shaded section bands and the structure mean printed above each band.
