# Psychology research note template

Use this as a question set, not a licence to fill gaps. Replace every placeholder before submitting the resulting document.
Set the new document's type to `research`, its status to `reference`, and its review interval to 365 days.
Add real `sources` mappings to the resulting document's frontmatter; retain this template's own metadata when editing the template.

## Study design and marketing-myth check

- {{source_kind}}: peer-reviewed empirical paper, review, meta-analysis, practitioner book or opinion. A publisher listing is not a full-book review.
- {{design}}: experimental, correlational, observational or theoretical; identify manipulation, comparator and outcome.
- {{sample}}: participants versus stimuli, recruitment, countries, category and exclusions; do not confuse asset count with respondent count.
- {{effect_size}} and {{uncertainty}}: report only measures stated in material actually opened; otherwise **Unknown**.
- {{replication_status}}: name the replication or review examined. No search hit is not proof of non-replication.
- {{limits}}: external validity, alternative explanations and conflicting evidence.
- {{marketing_myth}}: identify the attractive claim the evidence does not support; include a Claims we must not make section.
