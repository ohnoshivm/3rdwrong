# Portfolio strategy

**Status: proposed.** The portfolio is the studio's first public product: it
must show decisions and application evidence, not just polished images. The
plan below describes intended work, not completed projects. Source: *Founding
Blueprint v1.0, 22 October 2026 ([founding source archive](archive/founding-blueprint-v1.0.md))*.

## Three flagship projects

| Project | Brief and intended scope | Capability to demonstrate | Evidence and honesty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| South Asian consumer brand, self-initiated | Select a specific product context and audience; invent a bounded fictional brief or make a clearly labelled self-initiated concept. Develop research framing, strategy, concept routes, coherent static identity and realistic applications. | Cultural research translated into original art direction; typography, packaging-facing design and system decisions. | Label self-initiated or fictional on every published piece. Never imply a commission, sales lift, consumer preference or client approval. State hypotheses and tests actually run. |
| Programmable identity for a fictional creative-technology product | Define a fictional product and the communication problem it solves. First build a coherent static identity; then add one programmable or generative component with clear rules and fallbacks. | Concept-led generative behaviour, code as a controlled design material, static-to-dynamic continuity and accessible digital output. | Label fictional. Explain the rules and show reproducible examples. Do not suggest the prototype shipped or that users responded to it. |
| Real independent cultural or consumer business | Seek a small, clearly scoped engagement with an operating organisation and written permission for any public case study. | Discovery, collaboration, constraints, production decisions and handover in a real setting. | Publish only with permission. Separate verified implementation evidence from stated hypotheses; record what cannot be disclosed. |

Each case should explain the challenge, research, strategy, alternatives,
selected idea, system, applications, implementation evidence, outcomes or
hypotheses and learning. Use the [case-study template](../templates/case-studies/case-study.md).

## Fewer-is-better fallback

If three complete projects would reduce the quality of the work, build one
exceptional flagship, one experimental prototype and one real client project
in progress. Keep the work in progress private until permission and
confidentiality are clear. A smaller, well-explained body of work is preferable
to filler or invented results.

## Sequence and selection

1. Choose briefs with a real audience, tension and constraint; avoid styling a
   generic product category without a decision to solve.
2. Define the problem and strategy before making a visual route.
3. Articulate materially different concepts before polishing applications.
4. Establish the static identity before adding a generative or motion layer.
5. Test the system against real-use constraints and document evidence limits.
6. Publish only when the case is honest, legible and permitted.

Portfolio quality is not a substitute for buyer validation. Track the
questions prospects ask, the evidence they trust and the work that changes
their decision.
