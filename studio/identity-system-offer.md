# The 3RDWRONG Identity System

Identity remains the **core offer** under
[D-0018](decisions/0018-design-engineering-studio.md): a focused strategic and
creative engagement taking a brand from unclear positioning to a distinctive,
deployable identity. The scope below is carried forward from superseded
[D-0002](decisions/0002-identity-system-scope.md) for founder review. Exclusions
are now governed by D-0018: websites and interactive/3D experiences are
available through the separately scoped [Digital Experience offer](digital-experience-offer.md),
not automatically included in this engagement. The historical exclusion list
below describes the standard identity package, not the studio's full practice.
Workflow, checkpoints, acceptance, handover and change handling remain proposed.
The additional delivery range and pilot guidance below are proposed from
*Founding Blueprint v1.0, 22 October 2026 ([founding source archive](archive/founding-blueprint-v1.0.md))*.

## Carried-forward identity scope

> Standard scope:
>
> 1. Brand discovery, competitor analysis, audience research, positioning.
> 2. One coherent strategic direction.
> 3. Identity concept development and design exploration.
> 4. Core visual system: logo, typography, colour, composition, image direction.
> 5. Verbal identity foundations.
> 6. Key applications and motion principles.
> 7. Brand guidelines, asset library, handover.
>
> Explicit exclusions: comprehensive websites; continuous social media production; advertising management; packaging engineering; major campaigns; complex 3D experiences. These may become separately priced extensions or partner services.

## Proposed delivery workflow

| Phase | Proposed work | Checkpoint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discover and diagnose | Review business, product, audience, existing identity, competitors and constraints | Confirm source inventory and open questions |
| Strategy | Synthesize evidence, define audience, positioning, promise boundaries and creative criteria | **Strategy sign-off** before visual concept work |
| Concept | Develop at least three conceptually distinct routes and critique each against strategy | Select one route for development; record decision and rationale |
| Identity and system | Build the selected idea into core visual and verbal elements; test in agreed contexts | **Visual sign-off** separate from strategy sign-off |
| Validate and deliver | Check agreed applications, prepare guidelines, assets and handover | Accept deliverables against written criteria |
| Learn | Capture what changed, what failed and what remains uncertain | Close-out review and permission check before public case use |

**Proposed duration:** approximately 4–6 weeks, assuming timely client
feedback, access to decision-makers and agreed source material. This is a
planning range, not a promise or historical delivery record.

See the [engagement method](../methods/identity-engagement/README.md). The
phases are proposed until a human approves them. A client may agree to a
modified sequence in writing where dependencies remain clear.

## Proposed review checkpoints

Strategy approval and visual approval are distinct decisions. Strategy approval
confirms audience, position, scope and criteria; it does not approve a logo or
visual route. Visual approval confirms a selected direction and specified
system; it does not silently reopen signed strategy. Capture approver, date,
version and requested changes. If a change alters strategy, pause and agree a
change request before proceeding.

## Proposed acceptance criteria

| Deliverable | Acceptance criteria |
| --- | --- |
| Discovery and research synthesis | Inputs, sources, interview notes or limitations are traceable; documented facts are separated from interpretation, hypothesis and unknowns |
| Strategy | One approved-by-client direction states audience, context, positioning, key difference to test, promise boundaries and design criteria |
| Concept routes | At least three materially different ideas are stated without relying on style adjectives; each includes rationale, risks and a rough visual sketch |
| Core visual identity | Selected route contains logo family, type rules, colour roles, composition principles and image direction with documented usage constraints |
| Verbal foundations | Agreed voice principles, message hierarchy and sample language; no unsupported factual claims |
| Applications and motion principles | Agreed examples demonstrate the system in use; movement is specified only where it serves the identity |
| Guidelines and asset library | Files follow the agreed formats and naming; usage rules, licensing notes, accessible digital colour combinations and handover index are included |

The client and studio confirm the exact application list, source-file formats,
language coverage and any third-party permissions before work begins.

Motion systems, packaging production, websites and extensive campaigns are
separately priced when agreed; they are not implied by the standard scope. A
smaller pilot may be proposed where it answers a bounded question. Never sell
the full identity system at a price that prevents careful work.

## Proposed handover structure

Deliver the agreed package in a client-owned or client-approved location. The
proposed asset library is:

```text
brand-name/
  01-guidelines/       PDF and editable source where agreed
  02-logos/            SVG, PDF, PNG; light, dark and monochrome variants
  03-type/             licensed files or links, licence notes, no unlicensed fonts
  04-colour/           colour values and profiles for agreed digital and print use
  05-imagery/          approved art direction and licensed image references
  06-applications/     agreed exports and source files where included
  07-motion/           principles, specs and files where included
  08-admin/            asset index, licence notes, approvals and archive manifest
```

Final formats depend on the selected applications and agreed production
vendors. Vector masters are supplied as SVG and PDF where supported; raster
exports include PNG, with colour profile and size stated. Editable source files
are included only when agreed and technically useful. Do not embed font files
unless their licences permit it. Record ownership and permitted use for every
third-party asset.

Guidelines should explain strategy summary, identity idea, logo construction
and clear-space rules, minimum sizes, typography and scripts, colour values and
contrast, composition, image direction, verbal foundations, motion principles,
application examples, accessibility, production notes, do/don't examples and
asset locations. Use [final-delivery checklist](../methods/quality/final-delivery-checklist.md)
before handover.

## Commercial and delivery defaults

Approved defaults ([D-0023](decisions/0023-commercial-and-delivery-defaults.md)), pre-filled in the proposal, SOW and MSA and
editable per project with founder approval. Offer pricing remains a
hypothesis.

- **Payment:** 50% to start, 30% on approval of the chosen direction, 20%
  before final files are released.
- **Feedback:** two consolidated rounds per phase (concept, refinement,
  applications); further rounds are change requests.
- **Warranty:** 30 days of free defect fixes after launch; longer support is
  a paid care plan.
- **Portfolio:** named portfolio use after public launch with client-approved
  case-study wording, unless the client opts out as confidential.
- **AI:** AI use in the process is disclosed; AI-generated material in final
  deliverables needs the client's written consent.

## Proposed changes and extensions

When a request crosses an exclusion or approved deliverable, describe the
requested work, why it is outside scope, dependencies, fee, schedule impact,
rights and acceptance criteria in a written change request. Obtain agreement
before starting. Examples: engineering a folding carton, building a website,
producing weekly social assets, running advertisements, creating a campaign or
commissioning complex 3D. The studio may propose a qualified partner, but no
partner service is included until separately agreed. If an extension is not
accepted, deliver the agreed scope and list the excluded request as an open
item.
