# Proposed studio roadmap

This is an internal sequence, not a promise to clients or a forecast of
results. The schedule bends for quality, not for indecision. Blueprint-derived
dates and work items are proposed from *Founding Blueprint v1.0, 22 October
2026 ([founding source archive](archive/founding-blueprint-v1.0.md))*. The current next strategic dependency is the verbal
and conceptual foundation required by [D-0012](decisions/0012-strategy-before-own-visual-identity.md).

## Next strategic sequence

1. Define and approve 3RDWRONGS's personality, cultural perspective, verbal
   identity and conceptual territory. This Phase 0 must precede its own visual
   identity exploration.
2. Develop 3RDWRONGS's own visual identity through at least three distinct
   concepts, critique and application tests; record founder approval as a
   decision before use.
3. Validate the initial ICP through 15–20 qualified buyer interviews,
   starting with emerging beauty and personal-care brands. Fashion and
   lifestyle are secondary; creative-tech/software remains secondary to the
   primary-market test. See the [initial ICP](initial-icp.md) and
   [D-0010](decisions/0010-beauty-personal-care-discovery-focus.md).
4. Build portfolio evidence in parallel only where it does not bypass the
   strategy and research gates.

## Proposed 90-day sequence

| Phase | Dates | Intended work | Exit criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | 22 Oct–4 Nov 2026 | Establish positioning and ICP hypotheses; define the own-brand verbal and conceptual foundation; set up the research library; review three reference cases; audit production skills; define the two-offer launch. | Working creative brief and category hypothesis; usable research library; defined deliverables; chosen production tools; documented evidence gaps. |
| Capability | 5–20 Nov 2026 | Develop the creative process; complete typography and composition studies; build the first flagship's strategy and distinct concept directions; begin buyer conversations. | At least one critically reviewed identity direction with relevant applications; written critique record; initial buyer feedback with sample limits. |
| Evidence | 21 Nov–20 Dec 2026 | Complete one flagship case study; prototype a programmable identity after a coherent static identity exists; pursue suitable pilot clients; develop 3RDWRONGS's own visual identity only after D-0012 Phase 0 approval. | One public-quality, honestly labelled case; one bounded technical prototype; a functioning prospect and learning log; own identity remains gated by explicit creative approval. |
| Market Entry | 21 Dec 2026–19 Jan 2027 | Publish a studio presence and permitted case studies; pursue qualified opportunities; deliver any signed pilot work; refine offer and operations from evidence. | A credible presence, tested offer, documented workflows and actual evidence of buyer interest or purchasing behaviour. |

The exit criteria are planning tests, not claims that demand or results will
exist by those dates. Move an item forward only when the underlying evidence
and approvals are present.

## Current seven-day sprint: 22–28 October 2026

The sprint is a proposed focus list, not a time-budget requirement. Do not
represent actions as complete until artefacts and evidence exist.

| Day | Focus | Deliverable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 22 Oct | Establish the strategic foundation, document name-clearance questions and select one primary graphic-design application | Positioning v0.1, repository foundation, research log, tool choice and open clearance questions |
| 23 Oct | Study Experimental Jetset and apply one principle in an original exercise | First project-intelligence card and exercise |
| 24 Oct | Study PORTO ROCHA / MASP and COLLINS / Spotify | Two further intelligence cards and a reusable reference-analysis template |
| 25 Oct | Complete three typography and composition exercises against a fictional brief | Exercise records, critique and revision evidence |
| 26 Oct | Build a consumer-category flagship brief and research ten credible competing brands | Flagship brief, linked category sample and evidence-labelled audit |
| 27 Oct | Identify up to twenty qualified prospects and request exploratory conversations | Private prospect list and any documented buyer evidence; no invented interviews |
| 28 Oct | Create three conceptually distinct flagship territories, compare them and plan week two | Concept review, any human route decision and a next-step plan |

These deliverables are intended outputs from the blueprint's seven-day plan,
not evidence that the work has happened. Prospect details and interview notes
belong in a non-public store chosen before collecting the first client or interview data.

## Dependencies and deferred work

- Founder approval of the own-brand verbal and conceptual foundation gates
  visual exploration ([D-0012](decisions/0012-strategy-before-own-visual-identity.md)).
- A real category sample and qualified interviews gate changes to the ICP and
  offer hypotheses.
- Choose a non-public store before the first client or interview data exists;
  keep personal data, third-party contracts, credentials and NDA-bound assets out of this public repository
  ([D-0017](decisions/0017-fully-open-repository.md)).
- Publish any real case only after explicit permission and evidence review.
- `case-study-architect` is a candidate next skill, deferred until a real case
  exists to evaluate it against.
