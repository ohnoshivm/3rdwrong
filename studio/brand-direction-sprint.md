# Brand Direction Sprint

**Status: hypothesis.** This proposed entry offer and its price range are
untested. Source: *Founding Blueprint v1.0, 22 October 2026 (private archive)*.
It is a bounded strategic engagement, not a discounted identity system.

## Intended buyer and use

An operating founder-led business with a specific launch, repositioning or
identity decision that needs direction before commissioning a larger system.
The sprint should be declined when the buyer expects a finished logo package,
unlimited exploration or a substitute for required research and stakeholder
alignment.

## Proposed scope

- Review the supplied business, product, audience and identity material.
- Hold a focused discovery conversation with the decision-maker.
- Identify the decision to resolve, source gaps and material constraints.
- Synthesize the evidence into one working strategic direction and its
  unresolved assumptions.
- Present two or three verbal creative territories with rationale and risks.
- Recommend a next step: proceed to a separately scoped identity system,
  gather evidence, or stop.

This offer does not include a finished logo, visual identity system, production
assets, packaging engineering, website, campaign, extensive audience research
or implementation support.

## Deliverables and acceptance

1. A concise discovery synthesis with source and evidence labels.
2. A strategy-on-a-page stating audience, problem, position, promise boundary
   and design criteria.
3. Two or three concept territories described in words, without treating
   styling variations as different ideas.
4. A recorded recommendation, open questions and proposed next scope.

Acceptance means the agreed files are delivered, sources and limitations are
visible, the client has a review conversation and any unresolved decision is
recorded. Acceptance does not require agreement with a recommendation or
promise a business result.

## Working assumptions to test

- **Duration hypothesis:** 5–10 working days, subject to access to the
  decision-maker and timely source material.
- **Price hypothesis:** held privately as a test range. This is an unvalidated hypothesis, not a published
  rate or evidence of willingness to pay.
- **Fit hypothesis:** A buyer with an urgent strategic decision may prefer a
  smaller paid step before committing to a complete identity system.

Test these assumptions through qualified enquiries, proposal decisions,
feedback on the scope and paid acceptance. Record non-purchase reasons as well
as successful conversions. Do not infer demand from praise or hypothetical
interest alone.

## Boundary with the flagship offer

The [Identity System](identity-system-offer.md) remains the flagship offer.
The sprint must produce a useful decision artefact on its own. Do not use it to
sell an under-scoped full identity project or quietly include excluded
production work. Two offers only are proposed in
[D-0013](decisions/0013-two-offer-launch.md).
