# D-0013: Two-offer launch

## Decision

Propose launching with two offers only: the [Brand Direction Sprint](../brand-direction-sprint.md)
and the [3RDWRONGS Identity System](../identity-system-offer.md). Do not publish
a fifteen-service menu.

## Status

Proposed. The offer structure and the sprint's scope, duration and price
hypothesis remain untested.

## Rationale

Two connected engagements can give buyers a bounded first decision and a
clearer path to a complete identity system while keeping delivery focused.
The studio has no client history to support a broader service catalogue.

## Alternatives considered

Publish a broad list of capabilities as separate services; offer only the
complete identity system; add named technology, campaign and production
services before demand and delivery capacity are known.

## Evidence

Proposed: *Founding Blueprint v1.0, 22 October 2026 (private archive)* and
founding brief. No enquiry or conversion evidence yet supports either offer.

## Consequences

Describe extensions as separately scoped, not as additional launch offers.
Test enquiry quality, buyer comprehension, acceptance and delivery fit for
both offers.

## Revision triggers

Review after qualified enquiry and delivery evidence shows that buyers need a
different first step or that an additional offer can be delivered reliably.
