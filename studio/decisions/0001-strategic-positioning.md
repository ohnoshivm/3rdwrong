# D-0001: Strategic positioning

## Decision

3RDWRONGS is positioned as: “A brand identity studio first. A living identity specialist by capability. A perception-led consultancy by method.” This is one coherent practice: original, coherent, culturally relevant brand identities and visual systems; living extensions where useful; and research-informed creative judgement.

## Status

Superseded by [D-0018](0018-design-engineering-studio.md) on 2026-10-23.
The original decision and approval text below are retained as history.

Approved. This record does not silently change the status of
any related hypothesis or proposed implementation detail.

## Rationale

Identity is the core discipline. Living identity describes a capability rather than a separate menu of services. Perception-led describes how choices are informed. Keeping these in one statement avoids presenting three unrelated offers.

## Alternatives considered

Separate the three phrases into separate service lines; lead with creative technology; claim a market niche before validating it.

## Evidence

Documented: founding brief, 2026-10-22; founder-authored [positioning thesis](../positioning.md), 2026-10-22. No market evidence, client work or results yet. The positioning is an approved strategic direction, not proof of differentiation.

## Consequences

Use identity work as the centre of the offer. Explain motion, interaction and research as capabilities in service of identity. Keep audience and competitive claims labelled as hypotheses until validated.

## Revision triggers

Revisit if discovery repeatedly shows a different urgent need or buyer, if delivery evidence contradicts the one-practice model, or if the founder approves a new direction through a superseding record.
