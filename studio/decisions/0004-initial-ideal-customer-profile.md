# D-0004: Initial ICP and secondary market

## Decision

Hypothesis: the initial primary audience is founder-led, emerging premium consumer and lifestyle businesses in India, especially beauty and personal care, with some commercial traction and a meaningful reason to invest in identity. Fashion and lifestyle are secondary discovery segments. Early-stage creative technology and software companies are a secondary ICP valuable for portfolio development, but must not dilute primary-market testing. See the founder-authored [initial ICP](../initial-icp.md).

## Status

Hypothesis. This record does not silently change the status of
any related hypothesis or proposed implementation detail.

## Rationale

This is the initial audience proposed in the founding brief. It gives discovery a bounded starting point while leaving room to disconfirm the segment.

## Alternatives considered

Treat the primary segment as proven; target all companies; elevate creative technology and software to equal priority before comparing evidence; include cultural and independent hospitality businesses; test India and international prospects equally without accounting for decision-maker access.

## Evidence

Hypothesis: founding brief, 2026-10-22; founder-authored [initial ICP](../initial-icp.md), 2026-10-22. No market evidence or discovery conversations are recorded yet. Test India and international prospects, prioritising accessible decision-makers; compare response and closing rates. See [ICP validation plan](../initial-icp.md).

## Consequences

Use the segments to recruit discovery conversations, not as public claims about fit or market size. Record budget, trigger, alternative and counterexample evidence.

## Revision triggers

Review after 15–20 qualified buyer interviews or sooner if strong disconfirming evidence appears. Retain, narrow or supersede only through a new decision.
