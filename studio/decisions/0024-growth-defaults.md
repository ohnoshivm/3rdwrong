# D-0024: Growth defaults

## Context

The Growth engine left the channel list, outbound volume and awards budget as
founder decisions. Content, outbound and proof work cannot be planned or
measured without them.

## Decision

1. **Channels: all four.** LinkedIn, Instagram, the studio journal on the
   studio website, and Behance / Dribbble / X. Cadence per channel stays the
   proposed starting cadence in [content](../../growth/content/README.md#channels-and-formats);
   it is confirmed or revised after four weeks of measured capacity.
2. **Outbound: 20 personalised first messages a week.** Each is researched and
   written by hand. First messages are never automated or batch-sent, and the
   existing [outbound compliance rules](../../growth/outbound/compliance.md)
   apply in full.
3. **Awards: free submissions only** until the studio website or a flagship
   project is ready. The paid awards budget is zero for now.

### Still open

These remain founder decisions:

- lead-scoring thresholds;
- partnership and referral incentives;
- the trigger for paid advertising;
- growth targets;
- the postal address used in commercial email.

## Status

Approved by the founder on 2026-10-23. Approval sets defaults; it does not
validate channel fit, reply rates or demand.

## Rationale

**Interpretation** — Being present on every channel the studio's buyers and
peers use gives the most discovery while the studio has no proof base. A fixed
weekly outbound number makes the pipeline predictable and measurable, and
hand-writing every message keeps it consistent with the studio's quality
claim. Paid awards need work strong enough to justify the fee; spending before
that would buy nothing.

## Alternatives considered

- **Two channels (journal and LinkedIn):** sustainable, but slower discovery
  among peers and visual buyers.
- **5–10 outbound messages a week:** the previous hypothesis; safer for
  capacity but a thinner pipeline.
- **A paid awards budget now:** premature without a flagship project.

## Evidence

**Documented** — Founder decisions, 2026-10-23 (founder decisions session).
**Unknown** — channel response, reply rates and the real weekly hours these
defaults require; none has been measured.

## Consequences

- **Capacity risk (Interpretation):** all four channels plus 20 researched
  first messages a week, alongside client work, is a heavy load for a
  founder-led studio. The earlier outbound hypothesis was 5–10 accounts a week.
  Quality wins over volume: the existing quality gates and the content minimum
  (one journal piece a month, one LinkedIn post a week) still apply when
  capacity drops, and a missed week is better than a weak post or a generic
  message.
- Track weekly hours on content and outbound in the
  [growth metrics](../../growth/metrics.md) so the four-week review has data.
- Award submissions record a zero fee; a paid entry needs a new founder
  decision.

## Revision triggers

- Content or outbound quality-checklist failures in two consecutive weeks.
- Missed cadence on any channel for three weeks.
- The four-week capacity review.
- The outbound reply rate after eight weeks.
- Client delivery dates put at risk by growth work.
- The studio website or a flagship project is ready (revisit the awards
  budget).
