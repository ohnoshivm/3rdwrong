# D-0018: Design-engineering studio

## Decision

Reposition 3RDWRONGS as a design-engineering studio: brand identity at the core,
digital experience as the second pillar, perception-led by method. Living
identity remains a capability across the practice. Design and Engineering are
the two flagship departments; [D-0019](0019-organisation-and-loading-policy.md)
records the organisation and loading policy.

This supersedes [D-0001](0001-strategic-positioning.md) and
[D-0002](0002-identity-system-scope.md). The Identity System remains the core
offer. Websites and interactive/3D experiences are available through a
separately scoped Digital Experience offer, rather than excluded from the
studio's practice. They are not automatically bundled into an identity project.
Detailed offer, delivery and pricing proposals still require founder review.

**Ambition, not fact:** become one of the world's leading design-engineering
studios within 6–12 months of this decision. This is a founder ambition, not a
claim of current standing, delivery capacity or a forecast.

## Status

Approved by the founder on 2026-10-23. Approval establishes strategic direction;
it does not validate demand, approve a visual identity, or promote related
proposals and hypotheses to approved policy.

## Rationale

**Documented** — Founder instruction, 2026-10-23, identifies the benchmark set in
[taste references](../../taste/README.md), including the
[swipe file](../../taste/swipe-file.md) and
[studio websites](../../taste/studio-websites.md), as context for the change.

**Interpretation** — Original identity carried into a carefully engineered
digital experience offers a stronger differentiation thesis than treating
engineering only as a supporting capability. The studio's own presence should
be the first inspectable proof of that thesis. References inform the quality
bar; their layouts, assets and brand identities are not templates to copy.

## Alternatives considered

- **Identity-first with engineering as a capability:** retain D-0001's narrower
  positioning. This keeps the initial offer focused but does not express the
  founder's intended second pillar.
- **Decide later:** defer repositioning until more evidence exists. This limits
  immediate scope expansion but delays building the intended proof and paired
  Design + Engineering practice. The founder chose to set direction now while
  keeping commercial assumptions unvalidated.

## Evidence

**Documented** — Founder instruction, 2026-10-23, approves this direction and
ambition. The reference library records aesthetic and experiential benchmarks;
it is not market evidence for 3RDWRONGS.

**Hypothesis** — Digital experience can be a meaningful differentiator for the
studio. Test through original prototypes, qualified buyer conversations,
proposals, delivery costs and actual outcomes.

**Unknown** — No market evidence yet establishes demand for digital-experience
work in the initial ICP. [D-0004](0004-initial-ideal-customer-profile.md) and
[D-0005](0005-pricing-hypothesis.md) remain unvalidated audience and identity
pricing hypotheses; neither establishes a Digital Experience buyer or price.
No client results or award standing are claimed.

## Consequences

- Revise positioning, ICP, offers, pricing, methods and departments for the
  two-pillar practice. Preserve the founder-authored v0.1
  [positioning](../positioning.md) and [ICP](../initial-icp.md); prepare proposals
  alongside them. D-0018 governs conflicts while those sources await revision.
- Use [positioning v0.2](../positioning-v0.2-proposal.md) and the
  [Digital Experience offer](../digital-experience-offer.md) as proposals for
  review. Revisit D-0013's proposed two-offer launch; the Brand Direction Sprint
  is not silently promoted or removed by this decision.
- Set the quality bar at award-level digital craft: original concepts,
  coherent identity, careful interaction and real engineering quality. Awards
  are targets, not acceptance substitutes or promised outcomes. Performance,
  accessibility and reduced-motion behaviour remain delivery criteria.
- Build the studio's own presence as proof after the verbal/conceptual and
  visual-identity sequence described in [D-0012](0012-strategy-before-own-visual-identity.md).
  D-0012 remains proposed; this decision does not approve its creative outputs.
- **Risks:** 3D/WebGL production and maintenance costs may exceed estimates;
  the second pillar can create scope creep; founder bandwidth limits delivery;
  specialist collaborators may be needed. Use early prototypes, explicit
  budgets, written scope, capacity checks and named review owners before
  making project commitments.
- Cascade methods and department implementation separately; this decision
  records the required revision, not evidence that migration is complete.

## Revision triggers

Review after qualified buyer discovery, the first paid Digital Experience
engagement, or evidence of repeated losses, poor margins, inaccessible or slow
experiences, scope overruns or unsustainable founder workload. Review the
ambition at six and twelve months using portfolio, delivery and buyer evidence;
revise positioning or scope through a new founder decision if the thesis fails.
