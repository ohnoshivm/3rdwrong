# D-0010: Beauty and personal-care discovery focus

## Decision

Propose beginning buyer discovery with emerging beauty and personal-care
brands. Keep fashion and lifestyle as secondary discovery segments. This is a
test focus, not a claim that beauty is the largest or strongest market.

## Status

Proposed. The choice has no market evidence yet and does not establish that
prospective buyers can afford the studio's work.

## Rationale

Packaging, perception, recognition, premium positioning and visual consistency
are closely tied to how these businesses compete. This gives discovery a
specific set of questions to investigate; it does not prove segment size,
urgency or willingness to pay.

## Alternatives considered

Start with fashion and lifestyle equally; test creative technology as a
primary segment; delay discovery until a market-size ranking is available.

## Evidence

Hypothesis: founder direction, 2026-10-22. No buyer interviews, affordability
signals or comparative segment evidence are recorded. See the founder-authored
[initial ICP](../initial-icp.md).

## Consequences

Recruit qualified emerging beauty and personal-care buyers first. Log role,
trigger, current alternative, budget signals and reasons for not buying.
Keep fashion and lifestyle visible as secondary comparison groups.

## Revision triggers

Review after 15–20 qualified buyer interviews. Change the focus sooner if
repeated disconfirming evidence shows a different segment has clearer need,
access or purchase behaviour.
