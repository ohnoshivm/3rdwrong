# D-0007: Own identity remains in exploration

## Decision

Keep the 3RDWRONG identity in exploration. Require a documented process and explicit founder creative approval recorded as a decision before calling any logo, typeface, colour, tagline or visual direction approved. The proposed brief is [3RDWRONG identity project brief](../../projects/internal/3rdwrongs-identity/brief.md).

## Status

Proposed. This record does not silently change the status of
any related hypothesis or proposed implementation detail.

## Rationale

The founding brief says the own visual identity is not approved and the previous direction was rejected. A decision gate prevents an unfinished exploration from being mistaken for company policy.

## Alternatives considered

Treat a current mock-up as approved; restart from black, heavy grotesk and neon; invent a meaning for the name to justify a route.

## Evidence

Documented: founding brief, 2026-10-22. The next direction and meaning of the name are unknown.

## Consequences

The project brief remains open on settled name meaning, logo, type, colour, tagline and name treatment. A proposed candidate interpretation is recorded in D-0014; it is not approved. Route exploration must lead to an explicit recorded approval.

## Revision triggers

Reconsider when at least three distinct routes have been critiqued and tested across applications, then the founder has made an explicit selection.
