# D-0003: Operating philosophy

## Decision

The approved operating philosophy is: Meaning before appearance. Distinctiveness before fashionable conformity. Systems before isolated artefacts. Expression with restraint. Originality through informed experimentation. Human creative judgment leads; AI expands capability. Evidence and honest critique matter more than impressive terminology. Exceptional execution matters more than the number of tools installed.

## Status

Approved. This record does not silently change the status of
any related hypothesis or proposed implementation detail.

## Rationale

These principles describe the intended order of judgement: understand meaning and context, make a distinct idea, build a system, experiment responsibly, and execute with care.

## Alternatives considered

Treat each principle as a service promise; adopt tools or current aesthetics as default proof of quality; turn every observation into a universal rule.

## Evidence

Documented: founding brief, 2026-10-22. The principles are approved statements of intent. They are not evidence of achieved client outcomes.

## Consequences

Methods may operationalise the principles as proposed working practices. New examples and tests must remain clearly marked as elaboration until approved.

## Revision triggers

Revisit if repeated project evidence reveals a contradiction between principles, or if the founder approves a changed operating philosophy.
