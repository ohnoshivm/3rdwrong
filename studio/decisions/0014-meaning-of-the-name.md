# D-0014: Meaning of the name

## Decision

Test the candidate interpretation that “3RDWRONGS” means **refusing the
obvious answer when a better one remains undiscovered**. The intended quality
is intelligent unconventionality, not being wrong for attention.

## Status

Proposed. This is a candidate interpretation, not a settled meaning, origin
story, tagline or approved external claim.

## Rationale

The interpretation could connect the name to considered creative challenge
without celebrating mistakes for their own sake. It must earn founder
alignment and audience comprehension before it influences the identity.

## Alternatives considered

Treat the name as having no interpretable meaning; invent a literal origin;
equate being wrong with rebellion or attention-seeking.

## Evidence

Proposed candidate from *Founding Blueprint v1.0, 22 October 2026 ([founding source archive](../archive/founding-blueprint-v1.0.md))*. No audience feedback or pronunciation assessment is recorded.
Trademark, domain and pronunciation checks are required before identity
investment.

## Consequences

The own-identity brief may test this reading among other territories but must
label it as a hypothesis. Do not invest in identity assets or tell an origin
story on this basis alone.

## Revision triggers

Revise or reject after founder alignment, audience feedback, and trademark,
domain and pronunciation checks inform the decision.
