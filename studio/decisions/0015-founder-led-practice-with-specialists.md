# D-0015: Founder-led practice with specialists

## Decision

Propose operating as a founder-led independent practice, bringing in
specialist collaborators when a project requires skills or capacity outside
the founder's responsible scope.

## Status

Proposed. The working model is not a claim about an existing team or
collaborator network.

## Rationale

A focused practice can preserve direct creative accountability while accessing
appropriate specialist expertise. It keeps client needs ahead of premature
team or service expansion.

## Alternatives considered

Present a permanent multidisciplinary team before one exists; hire before
repeated demand is understood; promise a broad service catalogue and solve
delivery gaps later.

## Evidence

Proposed: *Founding Blueprint v1.0, 22 October 2026 (private archive)* and the
founding brief's founder-led company description. No collaborator or staffing
capacity is represented as established.

## Consequences

Describe the practice honestly as founder-led. Scope partner roles, capability,
availability, rights and accountability for each engagement before promising
their work.

## Revision triggers

Consider permanent hires or service expansion only when repeated demand and
management capacity justify them and a new decision records the case.
