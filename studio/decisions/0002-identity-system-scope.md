# D-0002: Flagship offer scope and exclusions

## Decision

The flagship offer is The 3RDWRONGS Identity System: a focused strategic and creative engagement taking a brand from unclear positioning to a distinctive, deployable identity. Approved scope: (1) brand discovery, competitor analysis, audience research, positioning; (2) one coherent strategic direction; (3) identity concept development and design exploration; (4) core visual system: logo, typography, colour, composition, image direction; (5) verbal identity foundations; (6) key applications and motion principles; (7) brand guidelines, asset library, handover. Exclusions: comprehensive websites; continuous social media production; advertising management; packaging engineering; major campaigns; complex 3D experiences. These may become separately priced extensions or partner services.

## Rationale

A named end-to-end identity engagement makes the strategic and creative scope legible while distinguishing it from ongoing production and specialist engineering.

## Alternatives considered

Offer only a logo; bundle websites, campaigns and ongoing channel production into the flagship engagement; imply excluded work is included when a client asks for it.

## Evidence

Documented: founding brief, 2026-10-22. Workflow, checkpoints, acceptance criteria, handover specifics, timeline and price are not approved by the brief.

## Consequences

Proposals must state the exact applications and deliverables. Any extension needs separate scope, fee, rights, dependencies and acceptance. Operating details remain proposed until human approval.

## Revision triggers

Revisit after delivery and win/loss evidence shows a repeated scope mismatch, or when the founder approves an explicit offer revision.
