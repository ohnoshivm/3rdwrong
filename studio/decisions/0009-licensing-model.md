# D-0009: Licensing model

## Decision

MIT for code and CC BY 4.0 for original documentation, methods and skills. Reserve trademarks and brand assets. Do not relicense third-party material; retain its original rights and notices.

## Status

Approved by the founder on 2026-10-22 for publication. This approval covers the licensing model stated here and does not silently approve any related hypothesis or proposed implementation detail.

## Rationale

The layers have different reuse needs: code can be reused under a permissive software licence, while documentation and methods can be attributed. Brand identity and third-party assets need explicit rights boundaries.

## Alternatives considered

Apply one licence to every file; imply that public availability grants trademark rights; relicense research images or excerpts without permission.

## Evidence

Founder publication approval, 2026-10-22. The model applies as described, subject to the third-party exclusions and trademark reservation below.

## Consequences

Apply the policy described in [LICENSING.md](../../LICENSING.md); retain per-asset attribution and rights notes.

## Revision triggers

Revise if legal advice, asset provenance, or the founder's licensing decision changes.
