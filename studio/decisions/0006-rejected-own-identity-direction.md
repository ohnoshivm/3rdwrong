# D-0006: Rejected own-identity direction

## Decision

Reject the exploration that combined black backgrounds, heavy grotesk typography and neon accents for 3RDWRONGS.

## Status

Rejected. This record does not silently change the status of
any related hypothesis or proposed implementation detail.

## Rationale

The founding brief records the direction as too conventional. It reads as a familiar “edgy studio” category code and does not establish an original reason for the identity.

## Alternatives considered

Continue polishing that visual package; use it as a fallback; treat any one element as a preferred direction. None is supported by the rejection.

## Evidence

Documented: founding brief, 2026-10-22. This record does not establish what the next direction should be or what the name means.

## Consequences

Do not use this exploration as current guidance or present any own-identity logo, typeface, colour, tagline or visual direction as approved. Explore materially different ideas through an explicit process.

## Revision triggers

This rejection remains unless the founder explicitly reopens it through a new decision with evidence and rationale.
