# D-0011: AI and technology not primary positioning

## Decision

Propose keeping AI-first and technology-first claims out of 3RDWRONGS's
primary external positioning. They describe ways the studio may work, not a
standalone reason for a client to hire it.

## Status

Proposed. The boundary can be revisited when client evidence shows that a
specific technology capability is a buying reason.

## Rationale

The approved strategic direction makes brand identity the core discipline;
living identity is a capability and perception-led practice is a method.
Leading with tools or technology could obscure the client's identity problem
and invite complexity without a need.

## Alternatives considered

Lead with AI generation; position as a creative-technology studio; describe
technology only when it serves a defined identity decision.

## Evidence

Documented: approved direction in the founding brief, 2026-10-22. Interpretation:
technology should support creative choices rather than replace the reason for
the engagement. No buyer evidence tests this positioning boundary yet.

## Consequences

External descriptions lead with the identity problem, concept and system.
Describe AI and technology accurately in process or capability context, with
human review and no implication of autonomous creative approval.

## Revision triggers

Revisit if qualified buyers repeatedly cite a specific AI or technology
capability as the reason to engage and the studio can demonstrate the
capability responsibly.
