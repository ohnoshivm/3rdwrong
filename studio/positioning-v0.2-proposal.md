# Positioning v0.2 proposal

**Proposed thesis for founder review.** [D-0018](decisions/0018-design-engineering-studio.md)
approves the design-engineering direction, not all wording or claims below.
The founder-authored [v0.1 thesis](positioning.md) remains unchanged as a source;
D-0018 governs where it conflicts with that thesis.

## What changes

Replace “a brand identity studio first” as the studio category with
**a design-engineering studio, with brand identity at the core, digital
experience as the second pillar, living identity by capability, and
perception-led by method.** Design and Engineering become paired flagship
departments under [D-0019](decisions/0019-organisation-and-loading-policy.md).

**Proposed wording:** 3RDWRONGS creates distinctive identity systems and
studio-grade digital experiences for ambitious businesses. Original ideas
connect how a brand looks, moves and behaves with how people understand and
experience it. Engineering participates from the concept stage so the digital
expression is part of the identity rather than a later decoration.

The [Identity System](identity-system-offer.md) remains the core offer. The
[Digital Experience offer](digital-experience-offer.md) is proposed flagship #2:
websites and interactive/3D experiences grounded in an identity system, with
explicit scope and performance/accessibility criteria. Living identity remains
a capability, not a mandatory effect or an extra service line.

## Founder wording carried forward from v0.1

The following wording is retained from the founder's thesis where it remains
consistent with D-0018. These remain a proposed thesis and promise, not evidence
of results:

> Our work begins with understanding a business, its audience, its competitive environment, and the perceptions it needs to establish or change. We translate that understanding into original creative concepts and coherent identity systems that function across physical, digital, and moving environments.
>
> 3RDWRONGS exists to bridge the gap between the quality of a business and the quality of how it is perceived and experienced.
>
> Technology expands the expressive possibilities of our work. It does not determine our creative direction.
>
> We create distinctive brand identities built around a strong idea, designed as coherent systems, and made to work in the real world.

Strategic intelligence, creative authorship and system thinking remain the
competitive thesis. Extending them into engineered digital experiences is a
**Hypothesis**, not a proven competitive advantage. Portfolio and client
outcomes must establish it. The v0.1 rejection of high-volume logo production,
one aesthetic for every client and technology complexity for its own sake
still informs this proposal; full departments do not imply a full-service
marketing offer.

## Evidence and ambition

**Documented** — The founder approved the strategic direction on 2026-10-23 in
D-0018. The [taste library](../taste/README.md) supplies benchmarks, not buyer
validation or permission to imitate references.

**Hypothesis** — A coherent identity and carefully engineered digital presence
will be valued together by a repeatable buyer segment. Compare qualified
consumer/lifestyle and creative-tech/software conversations, proposal demand
and delivery economics before narrowing the promise.

**Unknown** — Demand, budget, procurement authority and willingness to pay for
Digital Experience in the existing ICP remain unvalidated.
[D-0004](decisions/0004-initial-ideal-customer-profile.md) and
[D-0005](decisions/0005-pricing-hypothesis.md) remain hypotheses.

**Ambition, not fact:** become one of the world's leading design-engineering
studios within 6–12 months. The [roadmap](roadmap.md) proposes evidence-building
milestones; neither a reputation nor an award is claimed.

## Open questions for founder review

- Which buyer, buying trigger and market should lead the revised ICP? Does
  creative-tech/software move from secondary to a more central position?
- Which website and interactive/3D project types can the studio deliver with
  current founder capacity and named specialist collaborators?
- Should the public promise explicitly include digital experience, and what
  minimum portfolio evidence must exist before publishing that promise?
- How should the Brand Direction Sprint fit alongside the two flagship offers?
  Revisit the still-proposed [D-0013](decisions/0013-two-offer-launch.md).
- Which pricing model, maintenance boundaries and performance budgets should
  become standard after prototypes and cost evidence exist?
- Does [D-0011](decisions/0011-ai-and-technology-not-primary-positioning.md)'s
  proposed external-language guardrail need revised wording to distinguish
  design-engineering from AI-first or complexity-first positioning?

Review this proposal alongside the ICP revision and offer scope, then record
founder approval or revision explicitly. Do not overwrite the v0.1 source.
