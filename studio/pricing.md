# Pricing hypotheses

**Hypothesis** — Both ranges are unvalidated working assumptions, not fixed fees, market evidence or commitments. The founder approved their publication, not their commercial validation.

- **Identity System:** ₹1.5–3.5 lakh client budget/price hypothesis. See [D-0005](decisions/0005-pricing-hypothesis.md).
- **Brand Direction Sprint:** ₹35,000–₹75,000 test range, 5–10 working days. See the [proposed sprint](brand-direction-sprint.md).

**Digital Experience — Hypothesis placeholder: to be set by founder.** No fee
or range is established for the [proposed second flagship](digital-experience-offer.md).
[D-0018](decisions/0018-design-engineering-studio.md) requires pricing review;
the identity and sprint ranges above are not validated prices for websites,
interactive experiences or 3D work. Test scope, specialist costs, maintenance
and willingness to pay before quoting.

The [founding blueprint's Basic project economics](archive/founding-blueprint-v1.0.md#basic-project-economics) explains the illustrative economics; its [Commercial operating rules](archive/founding-blueprint-v1.0.md#commercial-operating-rules) retain the possible payment structure. These are source assumptions, not accepted client terms.

Validate willingness and authority to pay in qualified buyer conversations, then revise from actual proposals, win/loss evidence, delivery costs and founder time. Keep personal interview and client records in the non-public store required by the [publication rules](../docs/public-private-boundary.md).
