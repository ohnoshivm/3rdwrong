## What we are building

3RDWRONG is an independent brand identity studio creating distinctive, intelligent, and expressive identities for ambitious businesses.

We are a **brand identity studio first, a living identity specialist by capability, and a perception-led consultancy by method.**

Our work begins with understanding a business, its audience, its competitive environment, and the perceptions it needs to establish or change. We translate that understanding into original creative concepts and coherent identity systems that function across physical, digital, and moving environments.

We believe an exceptional identity is more than an attractive collection of assets. It is a recognisable language that gives a brand its own character, creates meaningful associations, and remains coherent as the business evolves.

## The opportunity

Many emerging businesses develop strong products, promising services, or compelling ideas before developing an equally strong brand identity.

As they grow, their identity often becomes fragmented: different visual treatments across platforms, unclear positioning, inconsistent communication, and limited recognition.

At the same time, increasingly accessible design tools and AI-generated content make competent visual production easier to obtain.

Our strategic hypothesis is that businesses with serious growth ambitions will continue to value original creative thinking, distinctive expression, and carefully constructed systems.

**3RDWRONG exists to bridge the gap between the quality of a business and the quality of how it is perceived and experienced.**

## Our specialisation

Our core competence is brand identity design, strengthened by research, strategy, and creative technology.

We develop:

- Brand positioning and strategic creative direction.
- Original visual and verbal identities.
- Distinctive typography, graphic languages, and art direction.
- Coherent, adaptable identity systems.
- Motion principles and living identity capabilities.
- Real-world applications, guidelines, and implementation assets.

We approach branding as both a creative discipline and a problem of perception, recognition, and meaning.

Technology expands the expressive possibilities of our work. It does not determine our creative direction.

## Our competitive thesis

We aim to combine three capabilities that are often commissioned separately:

**Strategic intelligence:** Understanding what makes a brand relevant, distinctive, and meaningful.

**Creative authorship:** Developing original concepts and executing them with exceptional care.

**System thinking:** Creating identities that can extend beyond static assets while retaining a recognisable core.

Our ambition is to make this combination our signature capability. It is not yet a proven competitive advantage; our portfolio and client outcomes must establish that.

## What we will not become

We will not begin as a full-service marketing agency, a high-volume logo business, an AI-generated design service, or an experimental technology studio selling complexity for its own sake.

We will not impose one aesthetic on every client.

We will not confuse originality with visual noise, simplicity with quality, or impressive mockups with real design competence.

The standard engagement's explicit exclusions are recorded in [D-0002](decisions/0002-identity-system-scope.md) and the [offer specification](identity-system-offer.md).

## Our initial promise

**We create distinctive brand identities built around a strong idea, designed as coherent systems, and made to work in the real world.**

Our long-term ambition is to develop a recognisable body of work, original methodologies, outstanding creative judgment, and a reputation earned through execution rather than self-description.
