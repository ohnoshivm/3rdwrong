# 09 Learning

## Objective

Capture reusable learning after delivery without turning one project observation into a universal design rule.

## Required inputs

- Decision log, approved artefacts, validation notes and open issues.
- Client feedback or outcome data with permission and provenance.
- A retrospective template and any skill-evaluation record from this engagement.

## Activities

1. Compare initial hypotheses with what was observed, what was delivered and what remains unknown.
2. Ask which method step changed a decision, which created friction and which was skipped.
3. Separate project-specific facts from repeated patterns and from recommendations.
4. Record mistakes and counterexamples without blaming an individual or smoothing over a failure.
5. Choose the right learning destination: principle, research insight, method, template, automated check, skill or documented exception.
6. Assign an owner and review point to proposed changes; do not edit approved policy silently.

## Research methods

- Use a retrospective conversation plus artefact review, with client feedback consent.
- Compare versions and decision records to see what changed, not only what people remember.
- Use outcome data only where collection, attribution and permission are adequate.

## Deliverables

- A completed [retrospective](../../templates/learning/retrospective.md).
- Evidence-linked lessons with context, confidence and counterexamples.
- Proposed improvements routed to their destination and owner.
- A permission decision for any public case-study material.

## Quality criteria

- No fabricated client result or causal claim.
- One-off observations remain scoped to their context.
- Proposed changes do not overwrite approved decisions without review.
- Sensitive information is stored only in the private project record.

## Common failure modes

- Calling satisfaction a business result.
- Converting one exception into a universal rule.
- Retrospective notes that omit a failed approach or uncertainty.
- Publishing project detail without permission.

## Human approval

People involved confirm factual accuracy and confidentiality. A human owner decides whether a lesson changes studio practice. Client participation does not transfer ownership of internal method decisions.

## Completion conditions

- Lessons have a source, context, uncertainty and proposed destination.
- Any changes are reviewed and recorded in the relevant file or decision.
- The project is closed with permissions and future learning needs clear.
