# 08 Delivery

## Objective

Package the approved identity, guidance and rights information so the client can locate, use and maintain the agreed assets.

## Required inputs

- Written strategy and visual sign-offs with version references.
- Validated source and export files, asset rights notes and client delivery location.
- The agreed format list, contact and handover needs.

## Activities

1. Run the final-delivery checklist item by item and resolve omissions.
2. Export vector and raster assets to agreed formats, sizes and colour profiles.
3. Name files consistently by brand, asset, variant, dimensions and version; remove ambiguous finals.
4. Provide font licence notes and permitted acquisition path rather than transferring restricted font files.
5. Deliver guidelines with strategy, concept, usage rules, accessibility, production notes, examples and exceptions.
6. Walk the client through the library and answer use questions; log accepted deviations and permissions.
7. Archive the final package and a manifest in the agreed location; verify access before closing.

## Research methods

- Compare delivered files with the asset index and open them in relevant software.
- Verify vector scale, raster dimensions, colour space and required transparency.
- Confirm the delivery link and permissions with the named client recipient.

## Deliverables

- Final guideline PDF and editable source if agreed.
- Asset library in agreed formats with index and rights notes.
- Handover record: location, version, recipients, permissions and open items.
- Archive manifest and confirmation that approved files match their sign-offs.

## Quality criteria

- No missing fonts, linked images or unlicensed assets.
- File names, variants and colour profiles are understandable to the recipient.
- Guidelines cover the rules needed to make routine applications.
- Delivery permissions are limited to agreed recipients.
- Archive and handover records identify the exact final version.

## Common failure modes

- Delivering only mock-ups with no usable source or export files.
- Bundling fonts or third-party images without permission.
- Marking files “final-final” without version control.
- Assuming a sent link means the client can open the files.

## Human approval

The client confirms receipt and access; the studio records this separately from creative approval. Confirm any included editable files and ownership or licence terms before transfer. Do not publish work without permission.

## Completion conditions

- The checklist is completed or every exception is recorded.
- The client confirms receipt and knows where the current files live.
- Permissions, archive and close-out notes are complete.
