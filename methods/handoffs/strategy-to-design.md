# Handoff: strategy to design

Strategy hands Design a signed direction that concept work can be judged against. Happens at identity phase 03 → 04 and Digital Experience DX-01 → DX-02. Concept work does not start without it ([identity 03](../identity-engagement/03-strategy.md), [Design quality rule 1](../../studio/departments/design.md#quality-rules-and-gates)).

- **From:** Strategy / positioning and research-perception (maker: `agent:strategic-architect`, or `agent:ceo` on flagship work).
- **To:** Design / direction (receiver: `agent:creative-director`).
- **Acceptance checker:** `agent:creative-director` in a Design instance that did not help write the strategy, with independent `agent:ceo` or `agency:design-brand-guardian` using `skill:3w-identity-system-critic` to check the creative criteria. The browser-only `agent:creative-auditor` joins when rendered work exists.

## Artefact checklist (sender)

- [ ] Signed strategy: `projects/<codename>/strategy.md` from [strategy on a page](../../templates/strategies/strategy-on-a-page.md), with `C1`'s dated sign-off in `decisions.md` and the gate log.
- [ ] Discovery synthesis and diagnosis with evidence labels (Documented, Interpretation, Hypothesis, Unknown) and linked sources.
- [ ] Category-code audit with its sample scope and selection bias (identity work).
- [ ] Creative criteria, each phrased so a route can pass or fail it.
- [ ] What the identity or experience must say, enable and avoid; promise boundaries.
- [ ] Hypotheses concepts may illustrate but cannot resolve; open questions with owners.
- [ ] Strategy / culture-behaviour records from `skill:3w-behavioural-lens` or `skill:3w-narrative-analysis` where a behavioural mechanism or sourced story changes the direction; primary sources and limits included.
- [ ] Pre-client `evidence-synthesis` and `positioning-canvas` case results from `evals/cases/strategy/`, plus independent judgement and human spot-check, or an explicit unresolved quality gap.
- [ ] Audience tasks and success criteria (Digital Experience, from DX-01).
- [ ] Constraints: scripts and languages, channels, production limits, licences known so far.

## Acceptance checks (receiver)

- [ ] The strategy version and approver match the gate log; no draft strategy is being used to authorise concept work.
- [ ] Every criterion can be tested without visual adjectives.
- [ ] No visual route is implied or pre-approved by the strategy pack.
- [ ] Audience claims without observed evidence are labelled Hypothesis.
- [ ] The receiver can state the direction back in one sentence; if not, return it.

## Return conditions

Return to Strategy when criteria are untestable, audiences conflict, a factual promise lacks a source, or the sign-off is missing or stale. A material strategy change discovered during concept work also returns here and is recorded before concept work resumes.

## Record

One handoff row in `decisions.md` ([format](README.md#handoff-record)).
