# 07 QA

## Purpose

Find and fix defects before the client and the public do: visual,
accessibility, cross-browser, functional and content QA on the production
build.

## Roles and sign-off

- **Design** (design-qa): visual QA against art direction, responsive and
  identity coherence, UX audit.
- **Engineering** (engineering-qa-security): functional, accessibility,
  cross-browser and security checks.
- **Production:** content QA with the client's content owners.
- **Sign-off:** independent reviewers (no department reviews its own work
  alone); founder logs gate DX-07 when no critical or major defect is open.

## Inputs

- Feature-complete build with motion and scenes (DX-05, DX-06).
- Final content from the client.

## Steps

1. Run the [web QA checklist](../quality/web-qa-checklist.md) on the
   production build.
2. Inspect every template at 1440, 834 and 390 px wide plus the agreed extra
   widths; screenshot each and list concrete defects (spacing, alignment,
   hierarchy, contrast, wrapping, overflow, timing).
3. Test accessibility: automated checks, then manual keyboard, focus order and
   visibility, screen reader on one desktop and one mobile pairing, zoom to
   200 % and reflow at 320 CSS px, reduced motion.
4. Test the browser and device matrix from
   [web standards](../quality/web-standards.md#devices-and-browsers).
5. Exercise every form, CMS flow and integration, including errors.
6. Proofread content and check links, metadata, alt text and legal pages.
7. Log defects with severity using the
   [critique protocol](../quality/critique-protocol.md) scale; fix; re-inspect.

## Outputs

- QA log in `qa/README.md`; defect list with severity, owner and state;
  screenshots in the vault.

## Quality gate

- [ ] No open critical or major defect; minor defects have an agreed
      disposition.
- [ ] Every template inspected at three widths after the last fix.
- [ ] Accessibility checked manually as well as automatically.
- [ ] Matrix covered or gaps recorded.
- [ ] Content signed off by the client's content owner.

## Evidence required

Screenshots before and after fixes (vault), automated report outputs, manual
test notes, defect log.

## Failure modes

- Declaring QA complete from an automated score.
- Fixing a defect without re-inspecting the template.
- Testing only the browser the team uses.
- Leaving content QA to launch day.
