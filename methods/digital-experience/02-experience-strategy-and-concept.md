# 02 Experience strategy & concept

## Purpose

Decide the one idea the experience expresses, the structure that serves the
audience tasks, and the moments where craft and interaction carry meaning.

## Roles and sign-off

- **Design** (direction, experience-design): concept, sitemap, key journeys,
  signature moments.
- **Engineering** (creative-engineering): feasibility of each signature moment
  on the device mix; first budget estimates.
- **Strategy:** checks the concept against positioning and audience evidence.
- **Sign-off:** founder selects the concept; `C1` approves the concept and
  sitemap (gate DX-02). Concept approval does not approve art direction.

## Inputs

- DX-01 synthesis, content inventory and success criteria.
- Approved identity strategy and system.
- Reference principles from discovery.

## Steps

1. State the concept in one sentence that ties the identity idea to the
   audience tasks.
2. Explore two or three distinct routes in words and low-fidelity sketches
   before any polish; critique them against the brief with the
   [critique protocol](../quality/critique-protocol.md).
3. Define the sitemap, page templates and the primary journeys, each tied to a
   task.
4. Choose no more than a few signature moments (motion, 3D or interaction).
   For each: what it means, what it costs, and its reduced-motion and
   no-WebGL alternative.
5. Engineering rates feasibility and likely budget pressure for each moment.
6. Present the selected route with consequences, not a gallery.

## Outputs

- Concept statement and route rationale.
- Sitemap and template inventory; journey maps.
- Signature-moment list with purpose, fallback and feasibility rating.
- Updated risks.

## Quality gate

- [ ] The concept is one idea, traceable to the identity and the brief.
- [ ] Every page template serves a named task.
- [ ] Every signature moment has a purpose, a fallback and a feasibility note.
- [ ] The route is original; references informed principles only.
- [ ] Scope matches the signed page and scene counts.

## Evidence required

Critique record with verdict, route decision in `decisions.md`, and `C1`'s
approval with version.

## Failure modes

- Presenting many routes to avoid making a recommendation.
- Choosing spectacle that no audience task needs.
- Approving a concept with no plan for reduced motion or WebGL failure.
- A sitemap that silently exceeds the signed scope.
