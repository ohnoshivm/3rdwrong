# 3RDWRONG identity exploration brief

**Project status: exploration.** No visual direction is approved. This brief
sets a process, not a predetermined aesthetic.

## Background

3RDWRONG is an emerging founder-led independent design and branding company
with global ambitions. The own identity needs to explain the practice clearly
while remaining usable as the studio develops. No clients, case studies,
revenue, awards, team size or history are on record.

## Strategic inputs to express

Use the approved direction in [decision D-0001](../../../studio/decisions/0001-strategic-positioning.md) and the founder's working [positioning thesis](../../../studio/positioning.md):

- Brand identity is the studio's first discipline.
- Living identity is a capability used when motion, interaction, generative
  design, adaptive graphics or digital environments serve the concept.
- Perception-led work is a method informed by strategy, consumer research,
  behavioural science, visual perception, memory, cultural intelligence and
  design judgement. Evidence, assumptions and speculative psychology remain
  distinct.

The working thesis is that 3RDWRONG exists to bridge the gap between the
quality of a business and the quality of how it is perceived and experienced.
The founder's initial promise remains a hypothesis, not approved copy.

Use [decision D-0003](../../../studio/decisions/0003-operating-philosophy.md)
for the eight approved operating principles. The identity should express one
coherent practice, not three unrelated offers.

## What the rejected direction taught

[Decision D-0006](../../../studio/decisions/0006-rejected-own-identity-direction.md)
records the rejection of black backgrounds, heavy grotesk typography and neon
accents as too conventional. Together, these cues can read as the category code
of an “edgy studio” without giving this identity a specific reason to exist.
The familiar creative-agency aesthetic is also rejected as a default premise.
Avoid a derivative voice built from oversized grotesk typography, saturated
colour, algorithmic graphics or editorial layouts without a specific strategic
reason. This is a warning about predictable combinations, not a ban on any
colour, type category or individual device. Do not reuse the prior exploration
as a reference route or fallback.

## Mandatory Phase 0: verbal and conceptual foundation

Before any visual exploration, define and obtain explicit founder approval for
the studio's personality, cultural perspective, verbal identity and conceptual
territory. The gate is recorded in
[D-0012](../../../studio/decisions/0012-strategy-before-own-visual-identity.md).
The foundation must make the one-practice model and positioning thesis legible
without relying on a visual style. An approved strategy is not visual approval.

The candidate interpretation of the name in
[D-0014](../../../studio/decisions/0014-meaning-of-the-name.md) is only a
question for evaluation, not settled brand meaning. Trademark, domain and
pronunciation checks are required before identity investment.

## Audience for the identity

**Proposed audience:** founders and brand decision-makers considering an
identity engagement, plus collaborators who need to understand how the studio
works. Validate whether these are the right audiences through discovery. Do
not claim established recognition or audience response.

## Success criteria

A proposed route should:

- Explain the one-practice model without relying on an abstract slogan alone.
- Have an idea that can be stated without visual adjectives.
- Distinguish itself from the rejected category-code combination for a reason
  tied to the approved strategy.
- Work across a wordmark, a research or strategy document, a presentation,
  motion principles and a small digital avatar test.
- Remain legible and reproducible at small sizes and in one-colour output.
- Avoid implying a client history, result, team size or capability not on
  record.

These criteria are proposed for exploration and do not approve any route.

## Mandatory process

0. Verbal and conceptual foundation: define personality, cultural perspective,
   verbal identity and conceptual territory; record founder approval before
   proceeding.
1. Strategy: restate approved inputs and define open questions. Complete
   required trademark, domain and pronunciation checks before identity
   investment.
2. Concept: articulate at least three conceptually distinct routes in words;
   make rough sketches before refining any application.
3. Critique: test each route against the strategy, idea, distinctiveness,
   system and craft; record risks and evidence labels.
4. Application test: place each surviving route in the agreed identity
   applications, including a small-size and one-colour check.
5. Founder approval: the founder selects or rejects a route explicitly. Record
   the choice, rationale, date and approved artefact as a new decision before
   presenting any own-identity element as approved.

See the proposed [identity engagement method](../../../methods/identity-engagement/README.md).

## Not decided

No logo, typeface, colour, tagline, name treatment, motion behaviour or visual
direction is decided here. None may be described as approved until the process
above ends in an explicit recorded decision.

## Name meaning remains open

The meaning or origin of “3RDWRONG” is unknown in the available brief. The
candidate reading in D-0014 is proposed only. Do not present it as settled or
build an identity investment on it before founder alignment, audience feedback,
and trademark, domain and pronunciation checks. Do not invent an origin story.
