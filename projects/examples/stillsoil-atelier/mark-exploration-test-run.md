## Idea without visual adjectives

The mark keeps a measured interval inside the name, so the brand is signed by a held space between "Still" and "soil" rather than by an added symbol.

## Routes

| Route | Organising idea | Mechanism | Category borrowing and reason | First risk | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M1 Held interval | The pause is built into the name: a fixed, wider-than-normal interval between "Still" and "soil" in a single wordmark | The name is read in two beats; the interval repeats as a spacing unit across the system | Wordmark-led, a pattern that may be common in the category — Unknown without an audit | Reads as a typing error, or as two words, at small sizes; needs a small-size companion | Shortlisted |
| M2 Press mark | The trace a hand leaves when it stops on a bar: one shallow, off-centre indentation | Gives a symbol tied to the product's use, usable as a blind emboss on paper | Uses the product's own form; bar silhouettes may be a category code — Unknown | Generic soap-bar silhouette; likely weak as a registrable device; emboss untested | Shortlisted for comparison |
| M3 Open bracket | The interval as a device: two short uprights that bracket the product name on every item | The brand is the frame around each product name, which directly supports name-first variant reading | No category borrowing identified — Unknown | Brackets are a common typographic device; weak as a standalone favicon | Kept as a system device, not a mark |
| Rejected: S monogram in a circle | Style variant, not an idea | — | Initial-in-a-circle is a generic device | Fails the idea test and the registrability caution | Rejected — no organising idea |
