# Sales Pipeline Analyst

The studio lead for `pipeline-forecast`: use this tool to handle pipeline forecast work with a source-grounded draft for human review.

## What it does

- Pipeline Analyst Agent.
- Pipeline Velocity Analysis.
- **Qualified Opportunities**: Volume entering the pipe. Track by source, segment, and rep. Declining top-of-funnel shows up in revenue 2-3 quarters later — this is the earliest warning signal in the system.
- **Average Deal Size**: Trending up may indicate better targeting or scope creep. Trending down may indicate discounting pressure or market shift. Segment this ruthlessly — blended averages hide problems.
- **Win Rate**: Tracked by stage, by rep, by segment, by deal size, and over time. The most commonly misused metric in sales. Stage-level win rates reveal where deals actually die. Rep-level win rates reveal coaching opportunities. Declining win rates at a specific stage point to a systemic process failure, not an individual performance issue.

## When to use

- Start with this lead for the `pipeline-forecast` job in sales/pipeline-forecasting.
- Use when the studio needs pipeline forecast during a live opportunity or internal commercial decision.

## When not to use

- Outside `pipeline-forecast`; route to the matching job lead when another job owns the work.
- Do not use it to approve its own work or make an unapproved commercial commitment.

## Inputs it needs

- Approved scope, intended decision and the minimum relevant source material.
- The target, account, call, pricing context or pipeline data required by this job; redact or keep personal data in a private store.

## How to run it

1. Confirm the job, trigger and desired output; provide only approved, necessary inputs.
2. Invoke the tool with the prompt below and request evidence labels where claims or recommendations are made.
3. Review the draft against the source material; route decisions, external actions and approvals to the responsible human.

```text

```
