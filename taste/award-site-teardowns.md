# Award Site Teardowns (rickcreates.com) — primary taste resource

## Why it is a primary resource

**Documented** — Founder judgement, recorded 2026-10-22: the founder calls Award Site Teardowns “one of our driving forces” for website inspiration. This designation records the founder's taste and intended use; it is not a claim that the site is an authority or that its analysis is independently verified.

## What it covers

**Documented** — The site presents teardowns of award-winning websites from Awwwards and FWA. Its Site of the Day section says it covers Awwwards SOTD entries scoring at least 7.60 or FWA entries scoring at least 85 points. The site's sections are [Site of the Day](https://rickcreates.com/), [Site of the Month](https://rickcreates.com/month) and [Site of the Year](https://rickcreates.com/year); a Chinese version is available at [/zh](https://rickcreates.com/zh).

**Documented** — The Design filters are Graphic, Spatial, Cinematic and Instrument; Build filters are Canvas, Scroll and Realtime. Site of the Year entries listed include Bruno's Portfolio, Lando Norris, Food for Fish, Igloo Inc, Lusion v3, KPR, Persepolis Reimagined and Pioneer — Corn Revolutionized.

**Documented** — The site describes performance and asset data as measured in a real browser. Individual reports can include page weight to open the home page. The exact measurement procedure is not published in the material reviewed.

## How each report is structured

**Documented** — The example [Heron AI report](https://rickcreates.com/r/2026-10-12-heron-ai) uses Concept, Design, Motion, Engineering, Scores and Team, and Archive sections. It describes the site's premise and visual system, interaction and movement, implementation, award scores and credits, and archive context.

## How 3RDWRONG uses it

**Interpretation** — Use this as the default source for the weekly reference-analysis slot in the [taste practice](../methods/taste-practice.md): choose one Site of the Year or Site of the Month teardown and one Site of the Day teardown whose Design or Build filter relates to current work. Record the study in the [project study template](../templates/research/project-study.md) or as a short [inbox note](../inbox/README.md).

For each study, separate documented observations from interpretation, then extract:

- **Interpretation** — the design invariants and how they connect to the concept;
- **Interpretation** — the motion logic and what each interaction communicates;
- **Hypothesis** — engineering cost compared with the effect it creates, checked against the original site where possible;
- **Documented** — reported page weight and performance or asset data, while preserving the site's measurement caveat.

Always finish with an original exercise, following [creative guardrails](../methods/creative-guardrails.md). Apply a principle through different formal decisions; do not reproduce the studied site's layout, copy, assets, identity or distinctive interaction sequence.

## Limits and cautions

**Interpretation** — This is secondary analysis by a third party. Verify claims against the original site and primary creator sources before reusing them. Award juries reward a particular “awards-site” vocabulary; repeated exposure can encourage derivative voice and revive the conventional direction rejected in [D-0006](../studio/decisions/0006-rejected-own-identity-direction.md).

**Documented** — Award recognition does not establish commercial effectiveness. [D-0002](../studio/decisions/0002-identity-system-scope.md) still excludes comprehensive websites and complex 3D experiences from the standard offer. Treat this as a taste and craft reference, not a service specification.

Link to reports and original sites; never copy report prose or site material into our work. This note contains no images or copied assets.
