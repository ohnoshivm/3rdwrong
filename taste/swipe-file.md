# 3RDWRONG — World-Class Website Swipe File

This is the founder's three-tier master list, not a quality ranking beyond the founder judgements explicitly labelled below. Revisit links before each study: sites change.

## Start here: founder's top five

**Documented** — Founder judgement, 2026-10-22: [Lusion](#lusion), [Buttermax](#buttermax), [Unseen Studio](#unseen-studio), [We Are Büro](#we-are-büro) and [Oryzo AI](#oryzo-ai) are “the strongest starting points for studying how distinctive creative direction, brand identity, motion and engineering can work together.”

**Interpretation** — Treat this as a selection of useful studies, not independent evidence that any site is effective or suitable for a particular audience.

## How to use the swipe file

Pick an entry by the question or craft need. Capture the source, date, context, observable decision, interpretation and open question in the [inbox](../inbox/README.md) or [project study](../templates/research/project-study.md). End with an original exercise using [creative guardrails](../methods/creative-guardrails.md). Link, never copy prose or images.

## Tier 1 — Exceptional interactive and award-winning websites

### Igloo Inc

[Website](https://www.igloo.inc/) · [Award Site Teardown](https://rickcreates.com/r/2024-07-23-igloo-inc)

**Documented** — The teardown describes Igloo as a crypto consumer-brand company and its site as a scroll-led 3D ice world. Mesh3D credits the featured Igloo project to Bureaux and Abeto. **What to study — Interpretation:** how scrolling, environment and visual metaphor make brand architecture discoverable. **Unknown:** the official site returned no extractable page text in this review; its current behaviour and present-day credits were not independently checked.

### Oryzo AI

[Website](https://oryzo.ai/) · [Lusion](#lusion) · [Award Site Teardown](https://rickcreates.com/r/2026-04-13-oryzo-ai)

**Documented** — The official page says “Designed by Lusion” and frames Oryzo as a cork coaster presented through an AI-product parody; Lusion also lists Oryzo among its work. **What to study — Interpretation:** product storytelling, sustained visual metaphor, scroll pacing and how humour supports a brand idea. **Unknown:** the mock-product framing is what the page presents; whether any product exists beyond the campaign was not verified.

### The Symphony of Vines

[Website](https://symphonyofvines.unseen.co/) · [Unseen Studio](#unseen-studio)

**Documented** — The official project page calls it an interactive cinematic journey about a château's terroir and says it is brought to visitors by Unseen Studio. **What to study — Interpretation:** translating place and origin into chapters, motion and interaction. **Unknown:** specific technical stack, production process and accessibility/performance were not verified.

### We Are Büro

[Website](https://www.burocratik.com/)

**Documented** — The website identifies itself as a digital studio and presents its team and work. **What to study — Interpretation:** studio voice, navigation and how project work is framed as evidence of a practice. **Unknown:** its ranking claim is self-published; no independent ranking or effectiveness evidence was checked.

### The Monolith Project

[Website](https://themonolithproject.net/) · [CSSDA WOTY 2025 nominee list](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/woty2025)

**Documented** — CSSDA's official WOTY 2025 page lists The Monolith Project as a nominee. **What to study — Interpretation:** inspect the live page for the relationship between concept, visual scale and interface once readable. **Unknown:** the official site opened without extractable text; maker attribution and page behaviour were not confirmed here.

### Exat Typeface

[Website](https://exat.hottype.co/) · [CSSDA WOTY 2025 nominee list](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/woty2025)

**Documented** — The official page identifies Exat as a new typeface by Hot Type and offers a type tester, character set, design space, stylistic sets, OpenType features and language support. CSSDA lists it as a 2025 nominee. **What to study — Interpretation:** how a type specimen lets visitors test a type system through interaction and information hierarchy. **Unknown:** the studio/individual designers behind the typeface are not named in the page material reviewed.

### Bruno Simon

[Website](https://bruno-simon.com/) · [Award Site Teardown](https://rickcreates.com/r/2026-01-21-brunos-portfolio)

**Documented** — The site identifies Bruno Simon as a creative developer and his portfolio as a world visitors drive through; the teardown says Simon designed and built it alone. **What to study — Interpretation:** playful navigation, discovery, interface help and the trade-off between memorable exploration and access to portfolio information. **Unknown:** independent user research and accessibility quality; the teardown reports that reduced-motion preference is ignored.

### Santioni Spirits

[Website](https://santionispirits.com/) · [Award Site Teardown](https://rickcreates.com/r/2026-10-05-santioni-spirits)

**Documented** — The teardown describes a launch site for a drinks brand using a scroll-led 3D comic with a choice that changes later story elements. **What to study — Interpretation:** choice, colour and product storytelling across a linear narrative. **Unknown:** maker credits were not found in the reviewed materials; the official site extraction only returned a JavaScript prompt.

## Tier 2 — Exceptional creative studios and digital experiences

### Lando Norris

[Website](https://landonorris.com/) · [Award Site Teardown](https://rickcreates.com/r/2025-11-20-lando-norris)

**Documented** — The official page identifies Norris as a 2025 McLaren Formula 1 driver; the teardown describes the personal site as organised around his racing helmet. **What to study — Interpretation:** building a coherent identity thread from a recognisable personal artefact across long-form page sections. **Unknown:** the maker credits were not verified in the reviewed source text.

### Siena Film Foundation

[Website](https://www.siena.film/)

**Documented** — The site presents a film catalogue with titles, formats, years, runtimes and film credits. **What to study — Interpretation:** how a moving-image archive can use a cinematic interface while preserving useful catalogue information. **Unknown:** the site maker and the foundation's organisational details were not verified in the extracted page.

### KOKUYO — Curiosity is Life

[Website](https://kokuyo.life/en/) · [CSSDA WOTY 2025 nominee list](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/woty2025)

**Documented** — The official site presents KOKUYO business domains, culture, history and future scenarios; the CSSDA nominee list includes “KOKUYO Curiosity is Life”. **What to study — Interpretation:** connecting a broad corporate story to distinct business areas and future-facing content. **Unknown:** maker credits and implementation details were not verified in the reviewed page.

### OddCommon

[Website](https://www.oddcommon.com/)

**Documented** — OddCommon describes itself as an independent digital agency focused on expressing brands and products through digital experiences. **What to study — Interpretation:** the repeated “creating belief” idea, tone and agency positioning. **Unknown:** performance and client outcomes are not established by the agency's own description.

### Studio K95

[Website](https://k95.it/)

**Documented** — The site identifies K95 as a graphic, brand and communication studio in Catania, with brand and digital design work. **What to study — Interpretation:** typographic voice, graphic motion and how an Italian-language studio communicates its offer. **Unknown:** authorship of individual projects and technical implementation were not verified.

### Obys

[Website](https://obys.agency/)

**Documented** — The official portfolio presents projects and lists services including creative direction, web design/development, concept and identity. **What to study — Interpretation:** how case selection, service labels and visual presentation build a studio's own point of view. **Unknown:** project outcomes and detailed team attribution were not confirmed for each entry.

### MONOGRID

[Website](https://monogrid.com/)

**Documented** — The supplied official URL opened but the extracted page only showed a JavaScript requirement. **What to study — Interpretation:** return in a full browser to inspect how the studio presents digital experiences and work. **Unknown:** current services, project credits and interactive behaviour were not verified in this review.

### Immersive Garden

[Website](https://www.immersive-g.com/)

**Documented** — The supplied URL was checked but was inaccessible to the source reader. **What to study — Interpretation:** inspect the live site directly for how its project stories connect concept, interaction and visual craft. **Unknown:** maker identity, current content and page behaviour remain unverified here.

### Bleibtgleich

[Website](https://bleibtgleich.dev/)

**Documented** — The site identifies Maksym Bleibtgleich as a designer and developer based in Kyiv, working across websites, products and digital systems, with a stated focus on clarity, performance and usability. **What to study — Interpretation:** direct self-presentation, work indexing and the relationship between portfolio craft and practical usability. **Unknown:** the performance and usability claims are self-described, not independently measured.

## Research additions (2026-10-23)

The entries below are new references from a studio research pass. Use the transferable question as a prompt, not as a direction to reproduce the site's expression.

### Advertising agencies and culture

#### Wieden+Kennedy

[Website](https://www.wk.com/)

**What to study — Interpretation:** how taste-led work can be presented as part of a wider cultural point of view, with the work itself carrying the agency's character.

#### Mother London

[Website](https://www.motherlondon.com/)

**What to study — Interpretation:** how an attitude-first voice can make an agency recognisable before its services are listed.

#### Mischief

[Website](https://www.mischiefusa.com/)

**What to study — Interpretation:** how an earned-media idea can be designed to travel beyond its first placement; inspect the relationship between the stunt, the story and the audience's retelling.

#### VaynerMedia

[Website](https://vaynermedia.com/)

**What to study — Interpretation:** social-first distribution at scale as an operating model. **Avoid:** treating volume and channel presence as a substitute for a distinctive, ownable point of view.

### Brand systems and studios

#### Collins

[Website](https://www.wearecollins.com/)

**What to study — Interpretation:** how editorial selection and art direction can connect brand strategy to a legible experience across touchpoints.

#### DixonBaxi

[Website](https://dixonbaxi.com/)

**What to study — Interpretation:** how a brand system can be built to move across broadcast, screen and other media while retaining a recognisable core.

#### Gretel

[Website](https://gretelny.com/)

**What to study — Interpretation:** how identity systems for networks and streaming services make a broad content offer feel coherent across programmes and formats.

#### Koto

[Website](https://koto.studio/)

**What to study — Interpretation:** how a studio presents brand and digital work for a varied, international portfolio without losing a clear point of view.
