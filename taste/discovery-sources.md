# Inspiration discovery sources

## Standing rule

**Documented** — Founder working rule, 2026-10-22: whenever a task involves gathering inspiration or visual/interactive references, use the `taste/` folder. Start at [taste README](README.md), then use this discovery guide, [Award Site Teardowns](award-site-teardowns.md) and [Studio websites](studio-websites.md), as relevant; cite the specific source page used. This is a workflow instruction, not an approval of any source's taste, methods or claims.

## Awwwards — Sites of the Month

**Verified URL:** [Sites of the Month](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/sites_of_the_month/).

**Documented** — Awwwards lists a Sites of the Month award alongside Sites of the Day and Sites of the Year, with browsable website categories, tags and technologies.

**Best for — Interpretation:** discovering polished art direction, interaction and studio work. The founder's claim that it surfaces work before it becomes widely referenced is **Unknown**; no evidence for that timing claim was found.

**Use in a project — Interpretation:** choose a relevant award entry and the original live site. In an inbox note or [project study](../templates/research/project-study.md), record the source URL and date, the brief/context visible on the site, one interaction sequence, one visual decision, and a principle to test through different formal choices.

**Cautions — Interpretation:** selection and awards reflect a particular editorial and jury lens. Verify current behaviour against the original site; do not infer commercial results or usability from recognition.

**Unknown** — The award page alone does not establish whether its selections are early or predictive of wider reference trends. Accessibility, performance and mobile behaviour have not been independently tested here.

## The FWA

**Verified URL:** [The FWA](https://thefwa.com/).

**Documented** — The official root URL opened, but returned no readable page text in the reviewed browser capture.

**Best for — Unknown:** the founder's framing describes experimental interactive experiences, creative technology, digital campaigns and immersive storytelling. These specific categories and emphasis could not be verified from readable official page content in this review.

**Use in a project — Interpretation:** treat it as a discovery lead. Open a selected award entry and creator's original page; record the interaction or technical idea, what user or story need it serves, and one feasible original experiment in an inbox note or [project study](../templates/research/project-study.md). Mark the source and any observed behaviours directly.

**Cautions — Interpretation:** an award listing is not proof of effectiveness, accessibility or technical suitability. Check those qualities on the original page.

**Unknown** — The official site's current categories, award criteria and the founder's four-part description were not verifiable in readable page content.

## CSS Design Awards — Website of the Year 2025 nominees

**Verified URLs:** [WOTY 2025 nominees](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/woty2025) and [2025 winners](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/blog/2025-website-of-the-year-winners/430/).

**Documented** — The nominees page exists and presents 2025 Website of the Year nominees. The winners page identifies the top ten by score and category winners, including UI, UX and Innovation.

**Documented** — CSSDA's winners page describes its WOTY judging panel as an international collective of web designers, developers, founders and creative directors. The official criteria/methodology could not be confirmed from the reviewed CSSDA material; the site's scoring details should not be inferred from other CSS Awards domains.

**Best for — Interpretation:** comparing nominee approaches and asking how visual quality, usability and innovation appear to relate within specific examples. The founder's description as a concentrated source of major 2025 projects is supported by the curated nominee list; it does not establish a general quality ranking.

**Use in a project — Interpretation:** select two nominees with different approaches to a relevant task. In a [project study](../templates/research/project-study.md), note the task, visible interface decisions, interaction steps, responsive behaviour and your own keyboard/accessibility/performance checks. Keep separate observations from judgements.

**Cautions — Interpretation:** nomination and scores are award signals, not commercial outcomes or an accessibility audit. Compare against the original pages and the actual audience/task.

**Unknown** — The scoring weights and detailed 2025 judging criteria are not established by the opened nominee and winners pages.

## Mesh3D Gallery

**Verified URLs:** [Gallery](https://mesh3d.gallery/) and [About](https://mesh3d.gallery/about).

**Documented** — Mesh3D describes itself as a curated showcase of interactive, real-time Three.js experiences. It identifies selected projects and makers; its About page describes the gallery as covering WebGL, Three.js and interactive 3D experiences. Some entries link to creators, but implementation detail availability varies.

**Best for — Interpretation:** finding real-time 3D/web experiments and tracing a selected site's maker or technology where those details are published. The founder's claim that technical implementation details are available for selected projects is only partly supported; inspect each entry before relying on it.

**Use in a project — Interpretation:** capture one technique or interaction question in an inbox note, then follow maker/site links. In a [project study](../templates/research/project-study.md), record the visible effect, likely user purpose, source-published implementation evidence, device behaviour and a lighter fallback to prototype.

**Cautions — Interpretation:** a curated technical showcase is not a recommendation to add 3D. Check loading, input alternatives, reduced motion, keyboard access and mobile behaviour yourself; separate observed effect from inferred implementation.

**Unknown** — The gallery does not guarantee implementation detail for every example, and this review did not independently test its listed sites.

## Landing Love

**Verified URL:** [About Landing Love](https://www.landing.love/about/).

**Documented** — Landing Love describes its entries as recordings of real pages scrolling from top to bottom. It says that when a held site is redesigned, it is recorded again and the old capture remains, creating a version timeline.

**Best for — Interpretation:** studying page progression and comparing recorded versions where available. The founder's “recordings” claim is verified; preserved recordings document captured scrolling, not necessarily every interactive state.

**Use in a project — Interpretation:** compare the available recording with the current live site. In an inbox note or [project study](../templates/research/project-study.md), capture page sequence, section transitions, apparent hierarchy, version/date context and what the recording cannot show; then test the live page's interaction directly.

**Cautions — Interpretation:** recordings can be historical and cannot establish current behaviour, accessibility, responsive states or commercial performance. Follow the original link and check the current page.

**Unknown** — Capture dates and completeness may vary by entry; confirm the specific record's version context.

## Cross-source cautions

**Interpretation** — Award and curation platforms can reinforce a shared “awards-site” vocabulary. Uncritical repetition risks derivative voice and revives the conventional direction rejected in [D-0006](../studio/decisions/0006-rejected-own-identity-direction.md). These are taste references, not policy; [D-0002](../studio/decisions/0002-identity-system-scope.md) and the offer remain unchanged.

**Documented** — Awards do not establish commercial effectiveness. **Interpretation** — Awards also do not establish accessibility. Check commercial fit, accessibility, responsive behaviour and performance yourself. Link to sources and original work; never copy layouts, prose, imagery, assets, identity or distinctive interactions.

## How findings compound

**Interpretation** — Capture a useful observation and source link in the [inbox](../inbox/README.md), bring it to [weekly triage](../methods/learning-loop.md), then route a project-specific study to the [project-study template](../templates/research/project-study.md). Promote only a contextual, tested transferable lesson to a proposed principle through the learning loop. Keep evidence, interpretation and hypothesis distinct.
