# Studio websites — taste references only

## How to use this list

**Documented** — Founder instruction, 2026-10-22: **TASTE REFERENCE ONLY**. These are aesthetic and
experiential benchmarks, not policy or a change to the client offer. [D-0002](../studio/decisions/0002-identity-system-scope.md)
excludes comprehensive websites and complex 3D experiences from the standard engagement.
3RDWRONGS remains a brand identity studio, not a WebGL production company or an immersive-website offer.

**Interpretation** — “What to study” records the founder's selection rationale, not an independent
quality ranking. “Explore” gives questions for a later browser session, not findings from tests already run.
Use the [taste practice loop](../methods/taste-practice.md) to turn a reference into an original exercise.

**Documented** — Sources were opened on 2026-10-22 through text retrieval, with Jina Reader used for
Buttermax, Resn, Active Theory and selected production accounts. Metadata includes attempted URLs as well
as readable pages; failed access is recorded below. No images or site assets are stored here.

**Unknown** — Rendered visual QA, live interaction behaviour, sound playback, mobile performance,
keyboard/screen-reader accessibility and reduced-motion handling are unverified for every site.
An extracted control label establishes published interface text, not that the control works.

For every reference, use these **Hypothesis** prompts in a later browser analysis and retain contrary evidence:

- Entry: can a new visitor identify the studio and reach work before a loader or spectacle loses them?
- Navigation: what is clickable, draggable or scrollable; is there a clear route back and a keyboard equivalent?
- Typography: does type carry identity, information hierarchy, or both; what happens on a narrow screen?
- Art direction: which objects, imagery, materials, lighting and compositions belong specifically to this identity?
- Motion and sound: which concept explains each transition; are audio controls clear and persistent?
- Mobile and access: record loading and responsiveness on a named phone/network; test touch, focus,
  contrast, screen-reader order, muted use and `prefers-reduced-motion`. Do not infer success from awards.

## Primary benchmark

### Buttermax

- **Documented** — [Website](https://buttermax.net/); [Jordan Frand's production account](https://www.jordanfrand.com/buttermax).
  The retrieved homepage describes a digital studio combining design, motion and technology.
- **Interpretation** — Study memorable, ownable personality and branded 3D assets: the founder's benchmark
  for a visual universe with a recognisable attitude rather than a collection of effects.
- **Hypothesis** — Explore how the entry introduces its “buttery” idea; trace one device through navigation,
  type, materials and pointer response. Apply the shared mobile, sound and access prompts above.
- **Interpretation** — Transfer: derive an original object family and behaviour from an identity concept;
  test whether a static composition still carries that character before commissioning technology.
- **Unknown** — Current location, exact production budget and present-day implementation are unverified.
  The homepage was blank in one text reader; Jina returned content. Full visual experience unverified.

### Lusion

- **Documented** — [Website](https://lusion.co/): the studio describes 3D storytelling and interactive web
  production; its footer lists Bristol, United Kingdom. These are the studio's own service descriptions.
- **Interpretation** — Study sophisticated interactive engineering and 3D craft, especially whether
  technical decisions support a single art-directed idea.
- **Hypothesis** — Explore the route from opening scene to project evidence; compare type hierarchy with
  spatial imagery, material/light consistency and transition rules. Apply the shared mobile/sound/access prompts.
- **Interpretation** — Transfer: specify a coherent image and material language for identity applications;
  judge consistency and readability separately from technical complexity.
- **Unknown** — Team allocation, costs, current stack and real-device performance/accessibility are unverified.
  The presence of play/mute text does not prove working audio controls or reduced-motion alternatives.

### Unseen Studio

- **Documented** — [Website](https://unseen.co/) and [World](https://unseen.co/world/): brand, digital and
  motion studio with Bristol and London addresses; published text offers entry without audio and drag exploration.
- **Documented** — [Awwwards listing](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/unseen-studio) dates SOTD to 6 February
  2023. The separate February 2023 SOTM lead has an access qualification in the review log below.
- **Interpretation** — Study atmospheric art direction and immersive studio identity: a consistent mood
  that can connect the portfolio, studio voice and exploratory world.
- **Hypothesis** — Explore entry with/without audio, the relationship of World to project navigation,
  type over imagery, light/material continuity and motion pacing. Apply the shared mobile/access prompts.
- **Interpretation** — Transfer: define an atmosphere through original image direction, composition and
  motion principles that can also work in static identity applications.
- **Unknown** — Current experience versus the awarded 2023 version, production cost and tested access quality.

### Noomo Labs

  explicitly mentions sound effects. [Awwwards' portfolio element](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/3d-portfolio-noomo-labs)

- **Interpretation** — Study playful experimental creative technology; the founder's iridescent-material
  lead is an observation target, not a visually verified description in this pass.
- **Hypothesis** — Explore how the invitation to enter teaches navigation, how type anchors experimental
  objects, and whether material changes and sound communicate state. Apply the shared mobile/access prompts.
- **Interpretation** — Transfer: use one original material/behaviour rule to give a small identity exercise
  personality; assess whether play makes information clearer.
- **Unknown** — Studio location, specific iridescent treatment, current rendering stack, costs and live
  sound, mobile, reduced-motion and accessibility behaviour remain unverified.

### fromanother

- **Hypothesis** — Explore light as a motion rule; compare entry, navigation, typography and imagery across
  projects. Check the later video's account against the live site and shared mobile/sound/access prompts.
- **Interpretation** — Transfer: write concept-based motion rules and an editable content hierarchy for an identity system.
- **Unknown** — Exact identity launch date, cost and live implementation unverified. The two developer
  articles differ on reveal/transition mechanisms; source inspection is required before treating either as a specification.

## Buttermax origin note

**Documented** — [Jordan Frand](https://www.jordanfrand.com/buttermax) says Buttermax launched as an
Active Theory sub-brand in October 2023, that he produced the debut website, and that the absence of a
specific client brief gave designers, developers and 3D artists room to experiment. He describes liquid
WebGL animation, 3D devices and mouse-tracking interaction. This is a first-person production account,
not independent evidence of audience effectiveness or proof of the current site's internals.

**Interpretation** — The useful lesson is an ownable idea pursued across disciplines. The no-client-brief
context also limits transfer: self-expression is not evidence that the same production suits a client's needs.

## What these references share (Interpretation)

**Interpretation** — The founder's three interconnected assets are a way to analyse these references,
not approved studio policy, deliverables, or a selected visual direction:

1. **Interpretation — An unmistakable identity:** an original wordmark, typographic language and recognisable
   graphic elements that make attribution possible without relying on a fashionable background treatment.
2. **Interpretation — An original visual universe:** custom objects, imagery, materials, compositions and
   art direction specific to the identity; for 3RDWRONGS, original work must emerge from its own concept.
3. **Interpretation — A living expression:** motion, interaction and creative technology emerging from
   that concept. Static expression should retain the idea; added behaviour needs a communication purpose.

## What not to take from them

- **Unknown** — Budgets, project-specific team sizes, production time and maintenance costs are not established.
  Do not turn visible ambition into an inferred affordable baseline or copy another studio's service offer.
- **Interpretation** — Spectacle can trade against load time, battery use, comprehension and access. Test
  mobile performance, keyboard use, contrast, reduced motion and muted operation; awards do not settle these questions.
- **Interpretation** — Novelty alone is not a reason for an effect. Ask what the identity communicates
  after removing sound, expensive rendering and cinematic transitions.
- **Interpretation** — Many selections share an “awards-site” vocabulary: loaders, immersive entry scenes,
  large type, pointer effects and dramatic transitions. Converging on that vocabulary risks category sameness.
- **Documented** — [D-0006](../studio/decisions/0006-rejected-own-identity-direction.md) rejects the own-identity
  exploration combining black backgrounds, heavy grotesk typography and neon accents as conventional.
  This collection does not reopen it or approve a replacement direction.
- **Interpretation** — Study principles, not recognisable layouts, characters, objects, palettes or interaction
  sequences. Do not copy assets, download imagery into this repository or reconstruct another studio's identity.
