# People and roles

**Proposed.** Who does what in a founder-led studio that works with
collaborators and AI agents, mapped to the 11 departments of D-0019, and how
the studio would grow into hiring. Departments are accountability
structures, not headcount.

## Purpose

Make every responsibility owned by a named role, so work can be delegated to
a collaborator or agent without losing who decides.

## When it applies

Staffing every engagement (L5), briefing any collaborator, and every
quarterly review of the bench.

## Roles

### Founder / creative director

Holds every department head role until delegated. Always retained:

- strategic, creative and release approval (D-0019);
- price, proposal, contract and payment decisions;
- the client relationship at decision-maker level;
- the review queue and every `approved` status;
- vault ownership and access control.

### Collaborator roles

| Role | Department (group) | Typical work | Reports to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Strategist / researcher | Strategy (Delivery) | Discovery research, positioning inputs, interviews | Founder |
| Identity designer | Design (Craft) | Concept development, identity systems, guidelines | Founder as creative director |
| Experience designer | Design (Craft) | UX/UI, responsive layouts, interaction specs | Founder as creative director |
| Motion designer | Design (Craft) / Media (Delivery) | Motion principles and assets | Founder as creative director |
| Creative engineer | Engineering (Craft) | WebGL/3D, shaders, prototypes | Founder; engineering lead when appointed |
| Frontend engineer | Engineering (Craft) | Site implementation, CMS, performance, accessibility | Founder; engineering lead when appointed |
| Producer | Production (Delivery) | Timelines, client communication, handover | Founder |
| Media specialist | Media (Delivery) | Photography, film, sound as scoped assets | Design direction |
| Copywriter | Design (Craft) / Marketing (Growth) | Verbal identity, site copy, studio content | Founder |
| Bookkeeper and CA | Operations (Studio) | Books, returns, tax advice | Founder |
| Lawyer | Operations (Studio) | Template review, disputes | Founder |

Growth roles (Marketing, Advertising, Outbound, Sales) are defined by the
Growth engine; the founder holds Sales sign-off.

### AI agents

Agents draft, research, check and organise under the human responsibility
split in the repository's agent instructions. They hold no approval rights,
never access the vault's money files unless the founder grants it for a
specific task, and never contact clients.

## Inputs

D-0019 department map, the current bench (vault), project staffing needs.

## Steps

1. For each engagement, name the role holding each department's
   responsibilities, including where the founder holds several.
2. Fill gaps from the [collaborator network](collaborator-network.md).
3. Brief and review under the [working agreements](working-agreements.md).

### Later hiring path

Hire only when evidence shows a recurring need; record the decision.

1. **Signal.** The same role is booked on most projects for two consecutive
   quarters, or founder hours on one non-founder task stay high in weekly
   reviews.
2. **Retainer first.** Offer a trusted collaborator a monthly retainer for
   reserved capacity.
3. **First hire.** Usually a producer (frees founder time across every
   project) or a designer or engineer matching the strongest-selling pillar.
   Decide from the scorecard, not ambition.
4. **Before hiring:** written role, cash runway covering the salary for a
   founder-set number of months beyond the reserve, employment contract and
   statutory registrations reviewed by the lawyer and CA. Employment changes
   IP ownership (work made under a contract of service belongs to the
   employer under the Copyright Act, 1957, section 17), tax and compliance
   obligations.

## Outputs

A staffing line per engagement in the project plan; a bench review each
quarter.

## Quality gate

- [ ] Every department responsibility on an engagement has a named holder.
- [ ] Every collaborator has a signed agreement before access.
- [ ] Founder-retained decisions are not delegated.

## Metrics

Utilisation by role; share of projects using each collaborator role;
founder hours by category.

## Failure modes

- Unowned responsibilities ("someone will handle handover").
- A collaborator becoming the client's main contact without a decision.
- Hiring from optimism after one big project.
