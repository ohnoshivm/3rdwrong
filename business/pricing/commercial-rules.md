# Commercial rules

**Proposed.** Rules for discounts, negotiation, rush work and changes. They
protect the price the [pricing method](README.md) arrives at and make
exceptions deliberate. Percentages and amounts are founder decisions recorded
in the vault; this page holds the rules, not the numbers. Default terms
are approved in [D-0023](../../studio/decisions/0023-commercial-and-delivery-defaults.md).

## Purpose

Keep prices consistent across clients, keep margin intact under pressure and
give the founder a clear yes/no test for every concession.

## When it applies

Whenever a client asks for a lower price, a faster timeline, different payment
terms or a change of scope, from L3 Propose through L8 Care.

## Roles

Sales — pricing-deal-desk prepares the response; Operations — finance-legal
checks cash and terms; the **founder** approves every concession in writing.
Collaborators never negotiate price with a client.

## Inputs

The approved estimate and calculator output, the SOW, the client's request in
writing and the vault history of past concessions.

## Steps

### Default terms

Approved defaults ([D-0023](../../studio/decisions/0023-commercial-and-delivery-defaults.md)); varying one for a project needs the founder's
written approval:

- **Payment:** 50 / 30 / 20 for Identity System and Digital Experience
  projects — 50% to start, 30% on approval of the chosen direction, 20%
  before final files are released. Brand Direction Sprint terms are set per
  proposal.
- **Feedback:** two consolidated rounds per phase (concept, refinement,
  applications); further rounds are change requests (see below).
- **Warranty:** 30 days of free defect fixes after launch; longer support is
  a paid care plan.
- **Still open:** the cancellation fee, rates and margins (vault) and
  care-plan pricing remain founder decisions.

### Discounts

1. Do not discount the same scope. Respond to price pressure by moving to a
   smaller option or removing named deliverables.
2. Permitted price reductions, each needing founder approval and a recorded
   reason: payment in full upfront; a confirmed multi-project commitment;
   a cultural, non-profit or portfolio-strategic project the founder chooses
   to support; a public case-study commitment with clearance agreed in the
   SOW.
3. Show any reduction as a named line against the full price, so the full
   value stays visible on the invoice and on renewal.
4. Re-run the calculator with the reduced price. A NO-GO stays a no.

### Negotiation

1. Ask what is driving the request: budget cap, cash timing, internal
   approval, or a competing quote. Each has a different answer (smaller
   scope, different payment schedule, a business case, a clearer comparison).
2. Trade, never give: every concession gets something back — scope,
   timeline flexibility, upfront payment, a testimonial or case-study
   permission, a referral.
3. Decide the walk-away point before the conversation: the floor-margin price
   for the smallest acceptable scope.
4. Confirm every agreed change in writing within one working day and update
   the proposal and vault estimate.

### Rush pricing

1. Rush means a timeline shorter than the estimate allows at normal capacity,
   requiring overtime, displaced work or extra collaborators.
2. Price rush as a surcharge on the affected stages, set by the founder per
   project, plus the cost of any added collaborator. Record it in the vault.
3. Rush never removes quality gates: strategy and visual sign-offs, QA and
   accessibility checks stay.
4. Decline when rush would compromise another client's agreed dates.

### Change pricing

1. Anything that adds, alters or re-opens a deliverable, criterion, timeline
   or approved decision is a change ([change-order bundle](../../templates/bundles/change-order/)).
2. Estimate the change with the [estimation guide](estimation-guide.md) at
   the same rates; include management time and any rework.
3. Price it with the calculator; change work must meet the target margin.
4. No change work starts before written approval. Small changes the founder
   chooses to absorb are recorded as absorbed, with hours, so the margin
   review sees them.

## Outputs

Updated proposal or change order (anonymised, repository), updated estimate
and decision record (vault).

## Quality gate

- [ ] The client's request and reason are in writing.
- [ ] The concession trades for something, or the founder recorded why not.
- [ ] Calculator re-run on the new price; verdict recorded.
- [ ] Written confirmation sent within one working day.

## Metrics

Share of projects with a concession, average discount from full price,
absorbed change hours per project. Definitions in the [scorecard](../scorecard.md).

## Failure modes

- Discounting in the room to close; the next proposal starts lower.
- Doing small changes for free until they add up to an unpaid stage.
- Rush work accepted without checking other clients' dates.
