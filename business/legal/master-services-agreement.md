# Master services agreement

**Template, not legal advice — have a qualified lawyer in India review before use.**

**Proposed.** The umbrella agreement for every client engagement. Each
project is a statement of work (SOW) under it ([SOW bundle](../../templates/bundles/sow/)).
Fill `{{placeholders}}` in a vault copy only. [D-0023](../../studio/decisions/0023-commercial-and-delivery-defaults.md) resolved the
portfolio (7.2), AI (9) and dispute (13.3) choices and set the default
payment split, feedback rounds and defect-fix period; these are pre-filled and
can be varied per SOW with founder approval. The remaining clause choices
marked **[Option]** are founder decisions to make once, before the lawyer
review.
Context and signing steps: [Contracts and legal](README.md).

---

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is made on {{date}} between
**{{studio_legal_name}}**, {{studio_entity_and_address}} ("Studio"), and
**{{client_legal_name}}**, {{client_entity_and_address}} ("Client").

## 1. Structure

1.1 This Agreement sets the terms for services described in one or more
statements of work signed by both parties ("SOW"). Each SOW incorporates this
Agreement.

1.2 If an SOW conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement prevails unless
the SOW expressly states the clause it overrides.

1.3 A proposal is not part of the contract unless an SOW incorporates it.

## 2. Services and acceptance

2.1 The Studio will perform the services and provide the deliverables in each
SOW with reasonable skill and care consistent with professional design and
engineering practice.

2.2 The Client will review each deliverable within the review period in the
SOW and either accept it or list specific failures against the SOW's
acceptance criteria. A deliverable is accepted when the Client accepts it in
writing, uses it publicly or commercially, or does not respond within
{{deemed_acceptance_days}} working days after a written reminder.

2.3 A failure is a deliverable not meeting a stated criterion. A new
preference, direction or requirement is a change under clause 4.

2.4 Unless the SOW states otherwise, each phase (concept, refinement,
applications) includes two rounds of consolidated feedback. Further rounds
are changes under clause 4.

## 3. Client responsibilities

3.1 The Client will name one decision-maker and one consolidated feedback
contact, supply material, access and approvals by the SOW dates, and ensure
that material it supplies may lawfully be used for the services.

3.2 Studio dates move day-for-day with Client delays. If the Client delays a
project by more than {{pause_days}} consecutive days, the Studio may treat the
project as paused under clause 11.3.

## 4. Changes

4.1 Either party may request a change. The Studio will describe the effect on
scope, fees, timeline and acceptance criteria in a change order. A change
takes effect only when both parties approve the change order in writing.

## 5. Fees, invoices and payment

5.1 Fees, milestones and currency are set in each SOW. Fees exclude goods and
services tax and other indirect taxes, which the Studio adds where
applicable.

5.2 The Studio invoices at the SOW milestones. Unless the SOW states
otherwise, identity-system and digital-experience fees are invoiced 50% on
signature, 30% on approval of the chosen direction and 20% before final files
are released. Work starts when the deposit invoice is paid in cleared funds.

5.3 The Client pays each invoice within {{payment_terms_days}} days of the
invoice date, without set-off, to the account on the invoice.

5.4 Where Indian law requires the Client to deduct tax at source, the Client
will deduct it, deposit it on time and give the Studio the certificate the law
requires. Amounts correctly deducted and certified count as paid.

5.5 Overdue amounts carry interest at {{late_interest}} from the due date, or
at the rate required by law if higher, including under the Micro, Small and
Medium Enterprises Development Act, 2006 where it applies.

5.6 If any invoice is more than {{work_pause_days}} days overdue, the Studio
may pause work on written notice until payment; timelines extend by the
pause and any remobilisation time.

5.7 Third-party costs listed in the SOW (licences, stock, hosting, production)
are payable by the Client as stated there. Licences the Client will hold are
bought in the Client's name where possible.

## 6. Intellectual property

6.1 **Pre-existing material.** Each party keeps its material existing before
or created outside the SOW. Studio pre-existing material includes its tools,
code libraries, templates, methods, know-how and general skills.

6.2 **Assignment.** On receipt of full payment for an SOW, the Studio assigns
to the Client the copyright in the final deliverables the SOW lists as
assigned ("Final Deliverables"), for all media, worldwide, for the full term
of copyright. This assignment is made in writing under section 19 of the
Copyright Act, 1957. Until full payment, the Client has a revocable licence
to review and test the deliverables only.

6.3 **Licence to embedded Studio material.** Where Final Deliverables contain
Studio pre-existing material, the Studio grants the Client a non-exclusive,
perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free licence to use it as part of the Final
Deliverables. **[Option]** permit or exclude modification and sublicensing.

6.4 **Unselected work.** Concepts, routes, sketches and drafts not selected
remain the Studio's. The Studio will not offer an unselected concept to a
direct competitor of the Client for {{unselected_concept_months}} months.

6.5 **Third-party material.** Fonts, stock, open-source software and other
third-party material remain subject to their licences, listed in the SOW or
handover. The Studio does not assign rights it does not own.

6.6 **Moral rights.** To the extent the law permits, the Studio will not
object to reasonable modifications of Final Deliverables after handover, and
the Client will not attribute modified work to the Studio without consent.

6.7 **Trade marks.** Trade-mark searches, clearance and registration are the
Client's responsibility unless an SOW includes them.

## 7. Portfolio and publication

7.1 The Studio may record anonymised learning from the engagement in its
public knowledge base: method, process and lessons identified by a codename,
without the Client's name, people, contact details, money, confidential
information, client files or any combination that identifies the Client.

7.2 After the Client has publicly launched the work, the Studio may show the
Final Deliverables in its portfolio, award entries and talks, naming the
Client, unless the SOW records that the Client has opted out as a
confidential client. The Studio will not publish confidential information or
unreleased work.

7.3 The Studio will send the Client any named case study
{{case_study_review_days}} working days before publication. The Client
approves its wording before it is published.

## 8. Confidentiality and data

8.1 Each party will keep the other's confidential information confidential,
use it only for this Agreement and disclose it only to people who need it for
the services and are bound by written confidentiality at least as strict.
This survives for {{confidentiality_years}} years after the Agreement ends,
and for trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets.

8.2 Confidential information excludes information that is public through no
breach, already known to the recipient, independently developed, or lawfully
received from a third party without restriction. Disclosure required by law is
permitted with prompt notice where lawful.

8.3 Where the Studio processes personal data for the Client, it acts on the
Client's documented instructions, keeps the data secure, uses it only for the
services, deletes or returns it at the end of the SOW, and helps the Client
meet its obligations under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and
other applicable law.

## 9. Use of AI tools

9.1 The Studio may use AI-assisted tools in research, drafting and
production. It will not enter Client confidential information or personal
data into a tool that may train on it or disclose it, and it will disclose in
the handover which Final Deliverables contain AI-generated material and the
tool used.

9.2 The Studio discloses its use of AI tools in its process in the proposal
or SOW. AI-generated material appears in a Final Deliverable only with the
Client's prior written consent.

## 10. Warranties and liability

10.1 The Studio warrants that, to its knowledge, the Final Deliverables are
original to it or properly licensed and do not infringe third-party
copyright. The Client warrants the same for material it supplies.

10.2 The Studio does not warrant business results, search rankings, award
outcomes, or that a mark can be registered.

10.3 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, or loss of
profit, revenue or goodwill.

10.4 Each party's total liability under an SOW is capped at the fees paid and
payable under that SOW, except for unpaid fees, breach of confidentiality,
infringement indemnities and liability that cannot be limited by law.

10.5 Each party indemnifies the other against third-party claims that
material it supplied infringes that third party's intellectual property,
provided it is promptly notified and controls the defence.

10.6 For 30 days after launch (or after acceptance where there is no
launch), the Studio fixes at no charge defects where the Final Deliverables
do not meet the SOW's acceptance criteria. Support after that period is
available under a separate paid care plan.

## 11. Term and termination

11.1 This Agreement runs until ended by either party on {{termination_notice_days}}
days' written notice; open SOWs continue unless also ended.

11.2 Either party may end an SOW on written notice if the other materially
breaches it and does not cure within {{cure_days}} days.

11.3 If the Client ends an SOW for convenience, or a project stays paused for
more than {{pause_days}} days, the Client pays for work done to that date,
non-cancellable third-party costs and **[Option]** a cancellation fee of
{{cancellation_share}} of the remaining SOW fees. Deposits are not refundable
except for Studio breach.

11.4 On termination the Studio delivers work paid for, and clause 6.2 applies
to paid deliverables only. Clauses 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 12 and 13 survive.

## 12. General

12.1 The Studio is an independent contractor and may use vetted
collaborators bound by written confidentiality and IP assignment; the Studio
remains responsible for their work.

12.2 During each SOW and for {{non_solicit_months}} months after, neither
party will directly solicit for employment a person who worked on that SOW
for the other, without consent. General advertisements are not solicitation.

12.3 Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its
reasonable control; affected dates move accordingly.

12.4 Notices are in writing to the addresses in the SOW; email is sufficient.

12.5 This Agreement and its SOWs are the entire agreement on their subject.
Changes must be in writing signed by both parties. It may be signed
electronically and in counterparts.

## 13. Law and disputes

13.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of India.

13.2 The parties will first try to resolve a dispute by good-faith
discussion between decision-makers for {{negotiation_days}} days.

13.3 Unresolved disputes go to arbitration under the Arbitration and
Conciliation Act, 1996 by a sole arbitrator appointed by agreement **[or
under the rules of {{arbitral_institution}}]**. Seat: {{arbitration_seat}}.
Language: English. Courts at the seat have supervisory jurisdiction.

13.4 Either party may seek urgent interim relief from a competent court.

## Drafting notes

- Payment-term days, late interest, pause threshold and cancellation fee
  are founder decisions; record them in the vault template copy.
- [D-0023](../../studio/decisions/0023-commercial-and-delivery-defaults.md) defaults: 50 / 30 / 20 payment split (5.2), two feedback rounds per
  phase (2.4), named portfolio use after launch with a confidential opt-out
  and client-approved wording (7.2–7.3), disclosed AI use with written consent
  for AI-generated deliverables (9.2), 30 days of defect fixes (10.6) and
  arbitration by a sole arbitrator (13.3). `{{arbitration_seat}}` is the
  founder's city, filled once by the founder.
- Clause 7 governs external portfolio use only. In this repository client
  work stays codenamed and anonymised until cleared under
  [D-0021](../../studio/decisions/0021-client-work-public-anonymised.md).
- For cross-border clients read the cross-border notes in [Contracts and legal](README.md).
- Keep the SOW's acceptance criteria objective; clause 2 depends on them.
- The assignment in 6.2 meets section 19 of the Copyright Act only if the
  signed SOW identifies the Final Deliverables.
