# Mutual non-disclosure agreement

**Template, not legal advice — have a qualified lawyer in India review before use.**

**Proposed.** Use before discovery when either side will share confidential
information. Many prospects need none: offer it when material is genuinely
sensitive, and never let it delay a first conversation. Fill
`{{placeholders}}` in a vault copy only. Context: [Contracts and legal](README.md).

---

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement is made on {{date}} between
**{{studio_legal_name}}** ("Studio") and **{{counterparty_legal_name}}**
("Counterparty"), each a "Party".

## 1. Purpose

The Parties will share information to evaluate and, if agreed, carry out a
possible engagement concerning {{purpose}} (the "Purpose").

## 2. Confidential information

"Confidential Information" means non-public information disclosed by a Party
("Discloser") to the other ("Recipient") in any form that is marked
confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential,
including business plans, financial information, customer data, unreleased
products, designs and source code.

It excludes information that the Recipient shows (a) is or becomes public
through no breach; (b) was lawfully known to it before disclosure; (c) was
independently developed without use of the Confidential Information; or
(d) was lawfully received from a third party without a duty of
confidentiality.

## 3. Obligations

The Recipient will:

1. use Confidential Information only for the Purpose;
2. protect it with at least reasonable care and no less care than its own
   similar information;
3. disclose it only to its employees, collaborators and advisers who need it
   for the Purpose and are bound by written confidentiality obligations at
   least as protective as this Agreement, remaining responsible for them;
4. not enter it into any AI tool or service that may retain, train on or
   disclose it;
5. notify the Discloser promptly of any unauthorised use or disclosure.

## 4. Required disclosure

The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information when required by law or a
competent authority, after giving prompt notice where lawful and disclosing
only what is required.

## 5. Return and deletion

On request, or when the Purpose ends, the Recipient will return or delete the
Confidential Information, except copies held in routine backups or required
by law, which stay confidential.

## 6. Term

This Agreement covers disclosures made within {{disclosure_period_months}}
months of its date. Obligations last {{confidentiality_years}} years from each
disclosure, and for trade secrets as long as they remain trade secrets.

## 7. No other rights

No licence, partnership or obligation to proceed with any transaction is
created. All information is provided as is.

## 8. Remedies

Breach may cause harm that damages alone cannot remedy; the Discloser may
seek injunctive relief, including under the Specific Relief Act, 1963, in
addition to other remedies.

## 9. Law and disputes

Governed by the laws of India. Disputes not resolved by discussion within
{{negotiation_days}} days go to arbitration under the Arbitration and
Conciliation Act, 1996 by a sole arbitrator. Seat: {{arbitration_seat}}.
Language: English.

## 10. General

This is the entire agreement on its subject, may be changed only in writing
signed by both Parties, and may be signed electronically and in counterparts.

Signed for the Studio: {{studio_signatory}} · Signed for the Counterparty:
{{counterparty_signatory}}

## Drafting notes

- Keep the Purpose narrow; a broad Purpose restricts the studio's other work.
- A client's own NDA is acceptable if it is mutual or limited to their
  information, and does not restrict the studio from working in the sector.
- The NDA does not grant portfolio rights; those come from the MSA.
