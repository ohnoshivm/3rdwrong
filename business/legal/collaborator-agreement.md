# Collaborator agreement

**Template, not legal advice — have a qualified lawyer in India review before use.**

**Proposed.** The agreement every freelance collaborator signs before seeing
any client material. Each piece of work is then a written brief
("Work Order") under it. Fill `{{placeholders}}` in a vault copy only. Fees
live in the vault. Process: [Collaborator network](../people/collaborator-network.md).

Under the Copyright Act, 1957 a freelance collaborator owns the copyright in
what they create unless they assign it in writing (sections 17–19). Without
this agreement the studio cannot assign that work to a client.

---

This Collaborator Agreement is made on {{date}} between
**{{studio_legal_name}}** ("Studio") and **{{collaborator_legal_name}}**,
{{collaborator_address}} ("Collaborator").

## 1. Relationship

1.1 The Collaborator is an independent contractor, not an employee, partner or
agent of the Studio, and controls how and when the work is done within each
Work Order's deadlines and standards.

1.2 The Collaborator may work for others, subject to clauses 4 and 7.

1.3 The Collaborator will not contact Studio clients about Studio work except
as the Work Order allows, and will not represent themselves as able to bind
the Studio.

## 2. Work Orders

2.1 Each Work Order states the deliverables, standards, deadline, review
process, fee or rate, and expense rules. A Work Order binds when both parties
accept it in writing; email or the Studio's project tool is sufficient.

2.2 The Collaborator will tell the Studio as soon as they foresee missing a
deadline or standard.

## 3. Fees and payment

3.1 The Studio pays the fee in the Work Order within {{payment_days}} days of
receiving a correct invoice for accepted work, regardless of when the client
pays the Studio **[Option: or within a stated period after client payment —
founder decision]**.

3.2 The Collaborator is responsible for their own income tax and, if
registered, goods and services tax shown on their invoice. The Studio deducts
tax at source where the law requires and provides the certificate.

3.3 Expenses are reimbursed only if approved in writing in advance.

## 4. Confidentiality and client anonymity

4.1 Client identities, people, contact details, briefs, files, fees and
Studio pricing are confidential. The Collaborator will use them only for the
Work Order, will not disclose them, and will not enter them into any AI tool
or service that may retain, train on or disclose them.

4.2 The Collaborator will access client material only through the Studio's
private systems (the "vault" and the tools the Studio names), will not copy it
to personal or public storage, and will refer to clients by codename in any
shared or public channel.

4.3 These obligations survive for {{confidentiality_years}} years after the
Agreement ends, and for trade secrets as long as they remain trade secrets.

## 5. Intellectual property

5.1 **Assignment.** The Collaborator assigns to the Studio all copyright and
other intellectual property in everything they create under a Work Order
("Work Product"), including drafts and unused material, for all media and
modes of exploitation now known or later developed, worldwide, for the full
term of the rights. The assignment of each item takes effect on its creation
or, if the law requires, on payment of the Work Order fee. This assignment is
made in writing under section 19 of the Copyright Act, 1957.

5.2 The Collaborator will sign any further document reasonably needed to
record the assignment.

5.3 **Moral rights.** To the extent the law permits, the Collaborator agrees
not to assert moral rights against modification or uncredited use of the Work
Product by the Studio or its clients. The Studio will credit the Collaborator
where it publishes the work and credit is customary, unless the client
prohibits it.

5.4 **Pre-existing material.** If the Collaborator uses material they owned
before the Work Order, they list it in advance and grant the Studio and its
clients a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free, transferable licence to use it
as part of the Work Product.

5.5 **Third-party material and AI.** The Collaborator will not include fonts,
stock, code, samples or other third-party material without the Studio's
written approval of the licence, and will disclose any AI-generated material
and the tool used. Open-source code must be listed with its licence.

5.6 **Warranty.** The Collaborator warrants that the Work Product is their
original work or properly licensed and, to their knowledge, does not infringe
anyone's rights.

## 6. Portfolio

6.1 The Collaborator may show Work Product in their portfolio only after the
Studio has publicly released it, with credit to the Studio, and without
naming the client unless the Studio confirms in writing that the client is
named publicly. Unreleased and confidential work is never shown.

## 7. Non-solicitation

7.1 During the Agreement and for {{non_solicit_months}} months after the last
Work Order, the Collaborator will not directly solicit, for work of the kind
they did under a Work Order, a client they were introduced to through the
Studio. This clause protects the Studio's client relationships; it does not
restrict the Collaborator from practising their trade. **[Lawyer to confirm
enforceability under section 27 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872.]**

## 8. Data protection

8.1 Where Work Product involves personal data, the Collaborator processes it
only on the Studio's instructions, keeps it secure, and deletes it when the
Work Order ends.

## 9. Term and offboarding

9.1 Either party may end this Agreement on {{termination_notice_days}} days'
written notice; open Work Orders are completed or settled pro rata for
accepted work.

9.2 On ending, or on request, the Collaborator returns or deletes all client
and Studio material, confirms this in writing, and the Studio removes all
access. Clauses 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8 survive.

## 10. Law and disputes

Governed by the laws of India. Disputes not resolved by discussion within
{{negotiation_days}} days go to arbitration under the Arbitration and
Conciliation Act, 1996 by a sole arbitrator. Seat: {{arbitration_seat}}.
Language: English.

Signed for the Studio: {{studio_signatory}} · Signed by the Collaborator:
{{collaborator_signatory}}

## Drafting notes

- Disputes follow the studio default in [D-0023](../../studio/decisions/0023-commercial-and-delivery-defaults.md): arbitration, sole
  arbitrator, seated in the founder's city (`{{arbitration_seat}}`).
- Payment timing relative to client payment is a founder decision; paying on
  acceptance protects relationships with good collaborators.
- Commissioned photographs and some commissioned works have special
  first-ownership rules under section 17; the assignment covers them anyway.
- Collaborators outside India: add their tax residency details and check
  withholding and foreign-remittance rules with the chartered accountant.
