# Founder review queue

**Proposed.** How the founder approves documents in this repository. Agents
and collaborators draft; only the founder approves.

## Purpose

Turn a growing pile of proposed and hypothesis documents into a short,
ranked list the founder clears in regular 30-minute sessions, so the
operating system becomes approved policy in order of importance.

## When it applies

Every document whose frontmatter status is `proposed` or `hypothesis`. Inbox
notes are triaged separately ([inbox](../inbox/README.md)).

## Roles

Operations — knowledge-systems runs the queue and prepares each session. The
**founder** decides. An author may attend to answer questions; the author
does not decide.

## Inputs

The queue from `uv run python scripts/review_queue.py`, ranked:

1. operating-critical areas first, in this order: decisions, studio strategy
   and offers, business engine, methods, growth, skills, projects, templates,
   governance docs, everything else;
2. within an area, the oldest `last_reviewed` date first.

Use `--limit 8` to print one session's worth.

## Steps

1. **Prepare (Operations, before the session).** Print the top of the queue.
   For each item add a two-line note: what it changes and what approving it
   commits the studio to. Flag conflicts with decisions.
2. **Session (founder, 30 minutes).** Work top-down. Spend at most five
   minutes per item; if it needs longer, decide *revise* with the question to
   answer.
3. **Decide one of four:**

   | Decision | Meaning | What changes |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | **Approve** | The founder adopts it as current policy or method | `status: approved`, `approved_by`, `approved_on`, `approval_source`; strategic approvals also get a decision record |
   | **Revise** | Direction right, content not ready | Stays `proposed`; a specific change request with an owner and date |
   | **Park** | Not needed now | Stays as is; `last_reviewed` updated; a note says what would bring it back |
   | **Reject** | Wrong direction | `status: rejected` with a one-line reason; links to it are updated |

4. **Record.** The founder, or someone acting on the founder's explicit
   instruction, edits the frontmatter in a pull request that names the
   session date. Agents never set `approved` on their own initiative.
5. **Rebuild the index** with `uv run python scripts/build_index.py` and run
   the validator.

### What approval means

- Approval means the founder accepts the document as the studio's current
  way of working. It does not validate a hypothesis, prove a claim or
  promise a client anything.
- Approval of a template approves its structure, not any filled-in copy.
- Approval of a legal template requires the lawyer review recorded first.
- An approved document is changed only by a new proposal that goes through
  the queue again.

## Outputs

Updated frontmatter, decision records where needed, a one-line session log
in the weekly review.

## Quality gate

- [ ] Queue generated by the script, not by memory.
- [ ] Each decided item has one of the four decisions recorded.
- [ ] No item approved without `approved_by`, `approved_on` and source.
- [ ] Index rebuilt and validator passing after edits.

## Metrics

Items decided per session; review-queue age (see [scorecard](scorecard.md));
share of operating-critical documents approved.

## Failure modes

- Sessions skipped until the queue is too long to start.
- Approving whole folders at once without reading.
- "Revise" with no specific question, so the item returns unchanged.
- Treating approval as evidence that a hypothesis is true.
