## Standards carried forward

- Founder-led, targeted business development: a small set of prospects whose
  context the studio has researched; no generic capability blasts.
- Never send unsolicited speculative rebrands or public teardowns of a
  prospect's identity. Start with curiosity and evidence.
- Respect non-response and opt-outs.
- Prospect categories to test follow the [initial ICP](../studio/initial-icp.md)
  and the beauty and personal-care discovery focus
  ([D-0010](../studio/decisions/0010-beauty-personal-care-discovery-focus.md));
  partner agencies remain a category to test. These are hypotheses, not
  market claims.
- Under [D-0021](../studio/decisions/0021-client-work-public-anonymised.md), prospect identities, notes, deal values and outreach to named
  people live in the private vault; only anonymised, aggregated learning
  returns here.

## Objections to investigate

These are **Hypotheses**, not statements heard from buyers. Record the
prospect's actual words in the vault before adapting a response.

| Possible objection | Honest response and next question |
| --- | --- |
| “We already have a designer or agency.” | If the current support solves the problem, there is no reason to replace it. Ask whether a specific identity decision or capability remains uncovered. |
| “We only need a logo.” | A logo-only request may not need a full system. Ask what decision or application is failing; offer the Sprint only if a strategic direction would help. |
| “We need proof before hiring a new studio.” | Acknowledge the absence of client outcomes. Show the method and clearly labelled self-initiated work; do not imply that these demonstrate client results. |
| “We can generate the assets with AI.” | Agree that production is increasingly accessible. Explain that the offer focuses on the problem, original concept, coherent rules and real-use checks; make no performance claim. |
| “The full engagement is too much for this decision.” | Check whether a bounded Sprint can answer the immediate question, or recommend no engagement. |

Revise or remove these when conversation evidence contradicts them, through
the [win/loss review](../growth/sales/win-loss.md).
