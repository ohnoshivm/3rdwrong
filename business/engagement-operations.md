# Engagement operations

This public-safe operating guide is proposed. Confirm the details with each
client before relying on them. The proposed engagement rhythm and term
categories draw on *Founding Blueprint v1.0, 22 October 2026 ([founding source archive](../studio/archive/founding-blueprint-v1.0.md))*.

## Project rhythm

- Hold a weekly check-in while active work is underway. If no meeting is
  useful, send a short written update with progress, decisions needed, risks
  and next actions.
- Keep a decision log with date, item, options considered, decision-maker,
  rationale, artefact version and any unresolved point.
- Send review material with a clear question and response date agreed with the
  client. Do not treat silence as approval.
- Use one named contact to consolidate feedback. Strategy and visual reviews
  remain separate checkpoints.

## Client experience principles

- Explain what the next decision enables and what remains open.
- Label evidence, interpretation, hypothesis and unknown information.
- Tell the client when a request changes the agreed scope or depends on
  information not yet supplied.
- Present meaningful options with consequences, not a gallery of unexplained
  variants.
- Keep critique about the work, criteria and evidence rather than personal
  taste or status.

## Feedback rounds

The number and format of review rounds are not approved. Propose them in the
engagement scope and specify what each review covers. One consolidated set of
comments per checkpoint is preferred. New directions after sign-off or changes
to approved strategy require a written change request. Record unresolved
feedback and the response agreed with the client; never silently fold a new
brief into the old fee or acceptance criteria.

## Default term categories

For each engagement, document the categories that need agreement: fixed scope,
milestones, limited feedback rounds, agreed payment dates, responsibility for
third-party licences, change-request fees and explicit ownership. Exact terms
depend on the project and must be agreed before work starts; this guide does
not supply contract language or payment percentages.

The [AIGA Standard Form of Agreement for Design Services](https://www.aiga.org/sites/default/files/2023-11/Standardformofagreement_2022update.pdf)
can be used as a professional reference for contract architecture. Any
agreement should be adapted by an Indian legal professional before use. This
link is a reference, not a substitute for legal review.

## File-sharing hygiene

Client files never belong in the public repository. Choose a non-public store before any client or interview data exists;
use a client-approved secure sharing location with named access. Share only the
files needed for the current decision, review permissions periodically, and
remove access when it is no longer required. Avoid personal data in public
research notes. Check third-party asset licences and client permissions before
sharing or publishing.
