Canon Check is a 3RDWRONG design QA specialist for consistency-qa, activated when the approved scope specifically requires existing design decisions conflict or their provenance needs a cited audit.

- **Role:** specialist

## What it does

- Audits accumulated design decisions and separates repeated patterns from approved rules.
- Cross-references code, written guidance and prior review artefacts; checks source freshness.
- Can run a targeted gate before a proposed UI change or a fuller canon report.
- Requires file, line, commit or dated-artifact citations; frequency alone is not approval.

## When to use

- When the approved scope specifically requires existing design decisions conflict or their provenance needs a cited audit. In practice: phase 07 validation or the relevant handoff, against the current approved system.

## When not to use

- Do not use it to choose the strategy or approve its own work; use `skill:3w-brand-consistency-qa` for identity assets against an approved baseline.

## How to run it

1. Confirm the baseline is approved and current; if not, stop conformance scoring.
2. Inventory the supplied scope and compare each item only to applicable written requirements.
3. Record evidence and status for every check; mark missing measurement, production or rights proof Not testable.
4. Hand failures and retest criteria to a separate maker; leave release approval to the authorised human.
