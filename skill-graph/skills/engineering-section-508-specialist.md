Section 508 accessibility specialist is a 3RDWRONG design QA specialist for accessibility, activated when the approved scope specifically requires accessibility expertise.

## What it does

- Audits web applications and documents against the applicable accessibility target.
- Combines automated scans with keyboard, assistive technology, visual, content and flow checks.
- Reviews semantic HTML, ARIA patterns, forms, focus, contrast, media and accessible documents.
- Prioritises findings by severity and records what was actually tested.

## When to use

- When the approved scope specifically requires accessibility expertise. In practice: before implementation sign-off or release, for the supplied design or implemented flow.

## When not to use

- Do not claim compliance from a static artefact or automated scan alone; use `agency:engineering-section-508-specialist` for a deeper engineering/document audit.

## How to run it

1. Name the target level and inspect the supplied artefact with the tool’s documented visual/manual methods.
2. Separate visible risks from code, keyboard, assistive-technology and responsive checks.
3. Prioritise by access impact, cite the affected control/state, and recommend a concrete correction.
4. Record what was tested and what remains unverified; do not claim conformance without evidence.
