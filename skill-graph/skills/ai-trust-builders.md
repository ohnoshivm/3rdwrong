Ai-trust-builders is a 3RDWRONG design QA specialist for ux-audit, activated when the approved scope specifically requires designing or reviewing human oversight, transparency, consent, and trust signals in AI experiences.

## What it does

- Applies seven trust patterns: Caveat, Consent, Data Ownership, Disclosure, Footprints, Incognito Mode and Watermark.
- Matches pattern intensity to product context and stakes.
- Recommends the most relevant one to three patterns with trade-offs.
- Treats caveats as insufficient on their own and keeps consent distinct by purpose.

## When to use

- When the approved scope specifically requires designing or reviewing human oversight, transparency, consent, and trust signals in AI experiences. In practice: during experience design or validation, against the named user task and flow.

## When not to use

- Do not use it to certify accessibility or production readiness; use `skill:accessibility` for design-level access risks and `skill:reviewing-interface-quality` for rendered interface quality.
