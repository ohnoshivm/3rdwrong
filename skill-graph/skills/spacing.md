## What it does

- Enforces optical alignment, 4/8pt spatial rhythm, and container/line-length constraints. Use when laying out screens, aligning icons to text, setting spacing, or reviewing uneven whitespace.
- reference/optical-offsets.md.
- Prefer fewer spacing tokens: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64. Larger jumps signal section breaks; smaller steps stay within one component.
- Group related controls with tighter internal spacing (4–8px) and separate unrelated groups with 16–24px.

## When to use

- A supplied layout has a measured alignment, spatial-rhythm or line-length defect.
- In practice: use it at Phase 04 Concept or Phase 05 Application when that specific operation is in the approved scope.

## When not to use

- For work outside this scope, route to `design-skills:design-elevation` for its related operation; do not use this tool as a general-purpose design lead.
