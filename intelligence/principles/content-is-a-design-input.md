# Let content constraints shape the identity

## Statement

**Interpretation** — Proposed principle: design around a recurring content constraint before choosing an
aesthetic treatment. The identity should help material belong together while protecting the information
that makes it useful.

## Evidence

**Documented** — Whitney's original account describes a graphic toolbox for institutional use.
Contemporary Spotify reporting identifies mixed artist photography as a practical treatment problem.
[Whitney source](https://www.jetset.nl/archive/whitney-museum-identity); [Spotify
source](https://www.fastcompany.com/3043547/spotifys-new-look-signals-its-identity-shift).

**Interpretation** — The [Whitney study](../projects/experimental-jetset-whitney.md) explores
content-responsive composition; the [Spotify study](../projects/collins-spotify.md) explores a common
image treatment. Accommodation and transformation are different responses to the same analytical starting
point: what material must the system handle?

## When it applies

**Hypothesis** — Consider this principle when images, product names or information lengths vary
repeatedly and the brand needs continuity without commissioning entirely new content for every
application.

## When it fails

**Interpretation** — Transforming content can destroy meaning or misrepresent a product. A rule that
needs favourable images and short English copy is too narrow if the client routinely supplies the
opposite.

## How to test it in a project

**Hypothesis** — Assemble a rights-cleared input set containing difficult crops, long names, absent
imagery and required scripts. Define which information must remain accurate. Compare untreated, adapted
and rejected inputs.

**Hypothesis** — Evaluate message retrieval, subject identification and family grouping separately.
Reject a treatment that improves grouping but damages essential product understanding; record an
exception instead of concealing the failure.

## What not to copy

**Interpretation** — Do not reuse the W or Spotify's duotone signature. Identify the actual constraint
and invent a different response. A borrowed treatment applied to different content can remain derivative.

## Application to 3RDWRONGS

**Hypothesis** — For a founder-led Indian consumer project, prototype with actual permitted assets and
language requirements. Include a low-expression alternative where a strong treatment would interfere with
product accuracy.

## Evidence classification

### Documented

**Documented** — The sources establish creator intent and a reported production problem, not a controlled
comparison of consumer outcomes.

### Interpretation

**Interpretation** — Content-led decision-making is our synthesis; it does not mean content must
determine every design choice.

### Hypothesis

**Hypothesis** — A constraint-led prototype could expose failures sooner than polished hero applications.
Compare its findings with a later operator trial.

### Unknown

**Unknown** — The best treatment, allowable exceptions and economic value for any new client require
project evidence.

## Open questions

- **Unknown** — Which input is most likely to break the proposed system in routine use?
- **Hypothesis** — Does the exception remain coherent enough to belong to the family? Test it alongside
the ideal examples.
