# Design for the next operator

## Statement

**Interpretation** — Proposed principle: a system is not resolved until the next operator's decisions and
responsibilities are visible. Creative authorship includes deciding what requires judgment, what can be
templated and what needs specialist support.

## Evidence

**Documented** — Whitney's designers describe active in-house interpretation and a manual. DIA's Adidas
page separately credits identity and motion, generative CMS and content capture. [Whitney
source](https://www.jetset.nl/archive/the-whitney-tapes); [Adidas
source](https://dia.tv/project/adidas-flagship-london/).

**Interpretation** — The [Whitney study](../projects/experimental-jetset-whitney.md) and [DIA
study](../agencies/dia-studio.md) show two operational boundaries: designing for another team and
collaborating across specialisms. Neither establishes a universal handover format.

## When it applies

**Hypothesis** — Use this principle when a founder, internal designer, freelancer or technical partner
must create new outputs after the originating studio's involvement ends.

## When it fails

**Interpretation** — Excessively prescriptive documentation can remove useful judgment; excessively open
tools can shift difficult decisions onto an unprepared client. A thick manual or sophisticated editor is
not proof of usable handover.

## How to test it in a project

**Hypothesis** — Name the operator and give them an unfamiliar but realistic task. Observe questions,
edits and failures without coaching. Record what requires clearer documentation, stronger defaults or a
simpler deliverable.

**Hypothesis** — For a technical extension, assign responsibility for inputs, content rights, asset
creation, software changes and maintenance. Reject an implementation plan whose ongoing owner or
dependency is unresolved.

## What not to copy

**Interpretation** — Do not imitate a 220-page manual as a status signal, or assume a small project needs
a generative CMS. Transfer the clarity of responsibilities, not the production scale.

## Application to 3RDWRONG

**Hypothesis** — A small consumer client might need a few robust templates and a concise example-led
guide. Establish that through observed use before proposing more elaborate tooling or specialist
extensions.

## Evidence classification

### Documented

**Documented** — The sources document creator accounts and collaboration credits. They do not supply
comparative operator error rates.

### Interpretation

**Interpretation** — Treating operator competence as a concept constraint is our practical inference, not
a recovered studio policy.

### Hypothesis

**Hypothesis** — A task rehearsal could reveal handover weaknesses before release. Compare the resulting
guide revisions with the operator's second independent attempt.

### Unknown

**Unknown** — Required training, support burden, software ownership and long-term cost remain
project-specific questions.

## Open questions

- **Unknown** — Who has the authority and competence to make exceptions after delivery?
- **Hypothesis** — Would a fixed asset library outperform an editable generator for the real operator?
Test the simpler alternative.
