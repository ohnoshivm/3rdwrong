# Studio Dumbar — DEMO across editions

## Source and attribution

**Documented** — Studio Dumbar/DEPT publishes DEMO 2022 and 2025 as visual identity, motion system, sonic
identity and creative coding work. [2022 account](https://studiodumbar.com/work/demo-2022); [2025
account](https://studiodumbar.com/work/demo-2025).

**Documented** — Its 2025 page identifies the festival as its initiative with named media partners. [2025
account](https://studiodumbar.com/work/demo-2025).

**Interpretation** — This is an identity study across editions, not an attribution of every exhibited
film to Studio Dumbar. Festival framing and contributed artworks must remain distinct.

## Business or creative problem

**Documented** — The 2022 account describes expansion from the first 2019 event to a nationwide edition
on 6 October 2022, with varied screen formats and locations. [2022
account](https://studiodumbar.com/work/demo-2022).

**Documented** — The 2025 account describes an international expansion. [2025
account](https://studiodumbar.com/work/demo-2025).

**Interpretation** — The useful challenge is maintaining an event identity while its content and
distribution change. A single preferred animation format would not resolve that operational problem.

## Research evidence

### Documented facts

**Documented** — For 2022 the identity was retained while colours moved from dark blue and orange/red
towards dark blue and yellow/green. A collaboration with Cavalry introduced a custom coding tool for
motion experimentation. [2022 account](https://studiodumbar.com/work/demo-2022).

**Documented** — For 2025 the studio describes a distorted globe, continuity of logo and typography, and
blue as the edition colour following green and red. It describes contrasting sonic textures and a sound
library developed in response to motion tests. [2025 account](https://studiodumbar.com/work/demo-2025).

**Documented** — The 2019 page states that a variable typeface changes through a self-developed script;
entered words receive animated forms, and cursor movement can distort the online logo. [Original
edition](https://studiodumbar.com/work/demo).

**Unknown** — The studio's legibility assertion is not an independent accessibility evaluation.

### Evidence boundary

**Unknown** — These studio narratives do not establish an independently measured relationship between the
festival identity and audience attention, comprehension or participation.

## Strategic or conceptual insight

**Interpretation** — A recurring identity needs both a stable recognisable core and a means of marking
the edition. Change can be allocated to selected dimensions rather than becoming an invitation to
redesign everything.

**Interpretation** — A motion festival presents a special case: motion is part of what the event offers.
That does not imply that intensive animation is useful for every brand whose communication happens to
appear on a screen.

## Visual and technical decisions

### Documented rules versus missing specification

**Documented** — The public pages specify edition-level colour change and retained core elements. [2022
account](https://studiodumbar.com/work/demo-2022); [2025
account](https://studiodumbar.com/work/demo-2025).

**Unknown** — Exact easing, loop duration, deformation bounds, frame rates and screen-safe typography
limits are not disclosed in those descriptions. Do not reverse-invent values and label them festival
rules.

### Interpretation of system relations

**Interpretation** — Treat motion as a vocabulary of behaviour whose outputs can be assessed at several
intensities. A highly expressive reveal and a readable information hold should answer different
communication tasks.

**Interpretation** — A repeated event can distribute change over time. A stable identifier and an
edition-specific parameter could make a series feel related without confusing the present edition with
its predecessor.

**Interpretation** — Different display formats imply different composition problems. Exporting a central
crop of one master animation is a proposition to test, not a sufficient definition of a responsive motion
system.

## Why these decisions may work

**Interpretation** — The tension between continuity and novelty can be managed by naming which dimensions
carry each role. This could make selection easier for operators and explanation clearer for clients.

**Interpretation** — A bold moving identity might also compete with the films it frames. Without an
audience study, visual intensity cannot be equated with effective festival navigation.

**Hypothesis** — An edition parameter could help returning viewers distinguish related events. Test
series grouping and edition identification separately; reject a route if viewers group the series but
repeatedly choose the wrong date.

## Transferable principles

**Interpretation** — Specify invariants and allowed variation before producing many assets. The [Whitney
study](experimental-jetset-whitney.md) explores a comparable distinction at the level of composition
rather than successive editions.

**Interpretation** — Define motion by a communication role, then specify its constraints. A distinctive
action should have a readable counterpart when movement is unavailable or inappropriate.

**Interpretation** — Treat tooling as support for authored judgment. The public evidence establishes
experimentation with a tool, not a universal requirement to build custom software for an identity.

## What not to copy

**Interpretation** — Do not copy DEMO's lettering, deformations, edition colours, globe or
festival-screen spectacle. An analogy to a launch or product range must generate its own vocabulary.

**Interpretation** — Do not confuse an event's deliberately expressive promotion with a suitable
interface for completing a task. Motion that interrupts essential reading would need revision even if the
film looks compelling.

## Evidence classification

### Documented

**Documented** — Attribution, selected retained elements, edition changes and the stated tool

### Interpretation

**Interpretation** — Stable core plus edition parameter is our abstraction. It is not a recovered full
guideline or a claim that the festival uses only one variable.

### Hypothesis

**Hypothesis** — A small family of behaviours can support multiple launch formats without losing
continuity. Test the least favourable aspect ratio and the longest message before approving the family.

### Unknown

**Unknown** — Budgets, exact authoring pipeline, full parameter ranges, operator training, accessibility
evaluation and independent audience outcomes remain unverified.

## Application to 3RDWRONG

**Hypothesis** — For a founder-led Indian consumer brand with repeated seasonal launches, test an
original stable identifier plus one controlled launch variable. Only use this structure if the actual
range and communication calendar warrant it.

**Hypothesis** — Deliver one short behaviour demonstration, one readable hold and one static counterpart
before proposing a larger motion package. Test the founder's ability to replace copy without breaking the
composition.

**Interpretation** — Distributed outdoor media partnerships and festival-scale production cannot be
assumed for a smaller business. A recurring static composition may provide enough continuity at lower
operational complexity.

**Hypothesis** — Use required language pairs and compressed mobile exports in a rehearsal. Reject
movement that obscures the name or product information, even if a large display version appears
attractive.

## Exercise

**Hypothesis** — Proposed exercise, adapted from Founding Blueprint v1.0, 22 October 2026 ([founding source archive](../../studio/archive/founding-blueprint-v1.0.md)): state what an original kinetic brand should communicate before choosing effects. Define five
motion principles covering initiation, transformation, timing, spatial behaviour and settling; give each
a semantic purpose and a rejection condition.

**Hypothesis** — Make three short applications, one static counterpart and one motion-reduced version
with the same real message. Specify your own timings as proposed values, never DEMO's undocumented
values. Critique readability, interruption and coherence before expanding production.

**Unknown** — This proposed exercise is unrun; no recognition or accessibility result is claimed.
