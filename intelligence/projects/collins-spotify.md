## Research evidence

### Documented facts

**Documented** — COLLINS describes a duotone treatment informed by music screen-printing and contrasting
colour pairs. [Studio case study](https://wearecollins.com/case-studies/spotify/).

**Documented** — The contemporary report describes abstract shapes derived from music controls, an
expanded palette and a tool called the Colorizer that automated duotone treatment using a selected
palette. It credits Brett Renfer with creating the software. [Contemporary
report](https://www.fastcompany.com/3043547/spotifys-new-look-signals-its-identity-shift).

### Evidence boundary

**Unknown** — Exact tool implementation, image-specific adjustment rules and time saved are not
established by these accounts. An automation story is not a reproducible software specification.

## Strategic or conceptual insight

**Interpretation** — The identity can operate on material the brand did not originate. A common treatment
changes how heterogeneous content belongs together without requiring a uniform photographic shoot.

**Interpretation** — A useful system answers a recurring production problem. The filter is interesting
because it can regularise variable inputs; adopting duotone purely as a fashionable look would miss that
rationale.

## Visual and technical decisions

### Documented mechanism

**Documented** — The present studio page calls the image-treatment approach Lens and describes its role
with third-party artist photography. [Studio case study](https://wearecollins.com/case-studies/spotify/).

**Unknown** — This retrospective terminology is not evidence that every current label or feature existed
in the 2015 implementation.

### Interpretation of system relations

**Interpretation** — Separate input, transformation and output: an image enters, a constrained treatment
is applied, and a composition places it alongside information. Each stage needs its own failure checks.

**Interpretation** — A common colour treatment cannot guarantee suitable cropping, correct subject
identification or readable overlaid text. The most useful local adaptation may be an exception rule that
protects the content.

**Interpretation** — Tooling could make a visual decision repeatable, but only if its defaults and export
options preserve the intended constraints. An automated mistake reproduced consistently remains a
mistake.

## Why these decisions may work

**Interpretation** — Repeated treatment could reduce accidental variation between externally supplied
images while allowing their subjects to remain diverse. A viewer might group the outputs as one
communication family.

**Interpretation** — A shared treatment can also flatten differences that matter. For a consumer product
whose colour is diagnostic, applying a brand filter to the product itself could misrepresent the offer.

**Hypothesis** — A constrained treatment could improve family resemblance without harming content
identification. Compare untreated and treated series, recording grouping, subject accuracy and
wrong-brand attribution separately.

## Transferable principles

**Interpretation** — Design the transformation rule before building the automation. Demonstrate that the
rule survives awkward inputs manually, then decide whether repeated use warrants a tool.

**Interpretation** — Distinguish coherence from sameness. A repeatable relation between imagery and brand
elements may be enough without making every image identical.

**Interpretation** — Compare the tool-based evidence in the [DIA study](../agencies/dia-studio.md). Both
references prompt questions about a repeatable operation, but a deterministic photo treatment is not the
same as selecting generative outputs.

## What not to copy

**Interpretation** — Do not reproduce Spotify's recognisable duotone combinations, burst shapes, graphic
compositions or music-cultural references. Another business needs a different formal answer to its own
input problem.

**Interpretation** — Do not use image transformation to bypass permission. Confirm the rights to edit and
publish each source image; this research document supplies none.

## Application to 3RDWRONGS

**Hypothesis** — For a founder-led Indian consumer brand with mixed supplier imagery, test an original
treatment on real product, portrait and contextual photographs. Include poor lighting and difficult crops
before recommending it.

**Hypothesis** — Begin with a small set of permitted compositions and one editable preset. Build a custom
generator only if repeated production and operator testing establish a useful need.

**Interpretation** — A global music platform's volume, image ecology and cultural position do not
transfer to a small skincare or fashion business. Preserve accurate skin, fabric and product colour where
those qualities matter to the communication.

**Hypothesis** — Test a static product sheet and a compressed small-screen image alongside an expressive
launch asset. A system that works only in a case-study film is not ready for handover.

## Exercise

**Hypothesis** — Proposed exercise: invent a consumer brand with an actual mixed-imagery problem, then
derive an original treatment distinct from Spotify. Use rights-cleared test material and include a
product whose colour must remain accurate.

**Hypothesis** — Make twelve outputs across three formats, including explicit exceptions where
transformation is unsuitable. Ask an unfamiliar operator to prepare a thirteenth output; record
corrections, product identification and family grouping separately. Build no custom software until the
manual rule survives this test.
