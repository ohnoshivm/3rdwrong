# Inbound handling

**Proposed.** How comments, DMs, site enquiries and platform messages are
answered, and how real enquiries are routed to L0 and L1.

## Purpose

Answer people promptly and well, and make sure no real enquiry is lost in a
social inbox.

## When it applies

Every channel the studio publishes on, the studio site's contact route and
platform messages (Behance, Dribbble, award platforms).

## Roles

| Role | Department | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First response | Marketing — content-social | Daily triage and replies |
| Routing | Sales — discovery-qualification | Takes enquiries into L0/L1 |
| **Founder** | Sign-off | Handles anything sensitive or public-facing disputes |

## Inputs

Notifications and inboxes for each channel.

## Steps

1. **Triage daily (15 minutes).** Sort each message into one of the types below.
2. **Respond** using the matching action. Be specific and human; no canned
   sales replies.
3. **Move enquiries off social.** For a real enquiry, thank them and offer a
   short call or a private channel (the studio's contact address or form).
   Do not discuss budget or project details in public comments.
4. **Record** the enquiry as an L0 lead with source `inbound-social`,
   `inbound-site` or `inbound-award` ([lead sources](../sales/lead-sources.md)).
5. **Close the loop** on unanswered threads in the Friday review.

| Type | Signal | Action | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Project enquiry | Asks about working together, availability or price | Thank, ask one clarifying question, offer a call; record L0 | Within 1 working day |
| Partner enquiry | Agency or studio asks about collaboration | As above; route to [partnerships](../partnerships-and-referrals.md) | Within 1 working day |
| Craft question | Asks how something was made | Answer generously if public; link the journal | Within 2 working days |
| Praise or comment | General appreciation | Thank briefly where natural; no obligation | When convenient |
| Criticism | Disagrees with the work or a view | Answer once, calmly, on substance; do not argue | Within 2 working days |
| Job or collaborator application | Wants to work with the studio | Polite reply; route to Operations — people-partners | Within 5 working days |
| Vendor pitch or spam | Selling to the studio | Ignore or decline; block abuse | — |
| Sensitive | Complaint from a client, legal, safety or personal-data request | Founder handles privately the same day | Same day |

## Outputs

Replies; L0 records in the vault; a weekly count of enquiries by channel.

## Quality gate

- [ ] Every project or partner enquiry answered within one working day.
- [ ] Enquiry moved to a private channel; nothing confidential in public.
- [ ] L0 record created with the right source code.
- [ ] Personal data from messages stored only in the vault.

## Metrics

First-response time; enquiries by channel; enquiry-to-qualified rate by
channel ([growth metrics](../metrics.md)).

## Failure modes

- Enquiries lost in DMs because nobody owns triage.
- Pricing or scoping in public comments.
- Defensive replies to criticism.
