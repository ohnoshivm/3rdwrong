# Content system

**Proposed.** What the studio publishes, where, how often, and why — derived
from the positioning and sized for a founder-led studio that does client work
first.

## Purpose

Make the studio's thinking and craft visible to the people it wants to work
with, so that when a trigger arrives (a launch, a repositioning) the studio is
already known and trusted. Content is proof of judgement, not volume.

## When it applies

All studio-owned publishing: social channels, platform profiles, award
profiles, the studio journal and site. Client channels are out of scope.

## Roles

| Role | Department | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Content lead | Marketing — content-social | Calendar, briefs, publishing, measurement |
| Voice | Marketing — studio-brand-voice | Voice and editorial quality (pending D-0012) |
| Production | Media; Design and Engineering for craft material | Visuals, motion, film, process captures |
| Craft review | Design or Engineering lead | Accuracy of every claim about craft or process |
| **Founder** | Sign-off | Approves anything that states a studio position or shows client work |

## Inputs

[Positioning](../../studio/positioning.md), the offer pages, the
[creative philosophy](../../studio/creative-philosophy.md), cleared project
material ([proof engine](../proof/README.md)), the [Taste Graph](../../skills/3w-taste-graph/SKILL.md) reference packs
and [taste references](../../taste/README.md), and the
[publication rules](../../docs/public-private-boundary.md).

## Steps

1. Choose pieces from the four pillars below, balanced over a month.
2. Brief, produce, review and publish through the
   [production workflow](production-workflow.md).
3. Route enquiries from comments and DMs through [inbound handling](inbound.md).
4. Measure monthly and adjust the channel mix quarterly.

### Content pillars

| Pillar | From the positioning | What it shows | Example pieces |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Identity systems | Core offer ([D-0018](../../studio/decisions/0018-design-engineering-studio.md)): distinctive, coherent identity systems that work in the real world | How an identity idea becomes rules that hold across uses | System breakdowns; "one rule, ten applications"; type and colour decisions explained |
| Digital experience | Second pillar ([D-0018](../../studio/decisions/0018-design-engineering-studio.md)): authored websites, motion, WebGL/3D grounded in an identity | Engineering as a design material, with performance and accessibility | Interaction studies; build notes; before/after performance; motion principles |
| Process and craft | Perception-led method; strategy before style | How the studio thinks: discovery, critique, alternatives, QA | Discovery frameworks; critique notes; rejected routes and why; QA checklists |
| Studio point of view | Creative philosophy; the studio's stance on originality and AI (D-0011) | Considered opinions that help buyers decide | Essays; reference teardowns that extract principles; notes on category conventions |

Rules: every piece belongs to one pillar; self-initiated or fictional work is
labelled as such; no client work appears without clearance under [D-0021](../../studio/decisions/0021-client-work-public-anonymised.md);
AI- and technology-first framing stays out of primary positioning (D-0011).

### Channels and formats

| Channel | What it is for | Formats | Starting cadence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Studio journal (studio site) | The canonical home of the studio's thinking; search and AI-answer visibility; linkable proof | Essays, case studies, build notes | 1 piece a month |
| LinkedIn (founder) | Reaching founders, brand and marketing leads, and agency partners; most direct link to buyers | Text posts with one image or carousel; short case notes; journal excerpts | 1–2 posts a week |
| Instagram (studio) | Visual proof for a visually literate audience; discovery by peers and brands | Carousels (system breakdowns), short motion pieces, process stills | 1–2 posts a week |
| X (founder) | Conversation with designers, engineers and creative technologists; craft credibility | Short build notes, motion clips, links to journal | 2–3 short posts a week |
| Behance / Dribbble | Portfolio discovery by peers, recruiters and some buyers | Full project pages (Behance); single strong frames or motion (Dribbble) | One Behance project per published case study; Dribbble 2 shots a month at most |
| Award-platform profiles | Credibility with peers and digital buyers; a record of recognition | Profile kept current; submissions per [awards](../proof/awards.md) | Updated with each submission |

The channel list is approved in [D-0024](../../studio/decisions/0024-growth-defaults.md): LinkedIn, Instagram, the studio
journal and Behance / Dribbble / X. The starting cadences above are proposed
defaults, confirmed or revised after four weeks of measured capacity. If
capacity drops, the minimum is one journal piece a month and one LinkedIn
post a week; a missed week is better than a weak post.

### Voice (placeholder, pending D-0012)

The studio's verbal identity is not decided (D-0012:
[strategy before own visual identity](../../studio/decisions/0012-strategy-before-own-visual-identity.md)).
Until it is, write to these interim constraints and do not invent a signature
voice:

- Plain, specific British English; short sentences; no jargon or hype words.
- Show the decision and its reason, not adjectives about the work.
- Evidence labels when making claims (**Documented**, **Interpretation**,
  **Hypothesis**); no invented results or statistics.
- No engagement bait, no fake urgency, no "hot takes" for reach.
- First person singular from the founder; "the studio" or "we" for studio
  pages. Revisit this section when D-0012 is decided.

## Outputs

Published pieces, a monthly content log with links and results, content
briefs in `growth/content/briefs/` (`content-brief` bundle).

## Quality gate

- [ ] Belongs to one pillar and one channel purpose.
- [ ] Clearance checked for any client material; fictional work labelled.
- [ ] Craft claims checked by the relevant lead.
- [ ] Voice constraints met; no invented results.
- [ ] Accessibility: alt text, captions on video, readable contrast.

## Metrics

Content output against plan, engagement quality (saves, shares, meaningful
replies) and enquiries attributed to content. See [growth metrics](../metrics.md).

## Failure modes

- Posting for the algorithm instead of the buyer.
- Imitating award-site aesthetics instead of showing the studio's thinking
  (see the [taste guardrail](../../taste/README.md#own-identity-guardrail)).
- Showing client work before clearance.
- Cadence the founder cannot sustain, followed by silence.
