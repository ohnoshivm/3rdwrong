# Advertising

**Proposed.** When paid media makes sense for a founder-led studio, and a
small, disciplined test framework for when it does. Budgets live in the vault.

## Purpose

Prevent spending money on reach before the studio has proof worth amplifying,
and make any later test cheap, bounded and learnable.

## When it applies

Before any paid placement for the studio itself: paid social, search ads,
sponsored posts, paid directory listings or award-platform promotion. Client
advertising is outside the studio's offers unless separately agreed.

## Roles

| Role | Department | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Test owner | Advertising — ad-creative-measurement | Designs the test, tracking and readback |
| Creative | Marketing; Media | Ad creative from existing proof |
| **Founder** | Sign-off | Approves the test, budget cap and stop rule (vault) |

## Inputs

Published proof; the sales system's conversion data; tracking on the studio
site; a budget cap decided in the vault.

## Steps

### The honest answer for now: not yet

**Interpretation:** the studio sells high-consideration, founder-led work to
a narrow ICP still being validated (D-0004). Buyers of that kind usually
decide through trust — referrals, work they have seen, conversation — and a
studio with few published case studies has little to amplify. Paid media is
therefore unlikely to be the best use of money or time yet.

Run a paid test only when **all** of these are true:

- [ ] At least two published case studies or strong labelled self-initiated
      projects in the pillar being promoted.
- [ ] The sales system works: leads are qualified, discovery converts to
      proposals, and the studio can say which sources win.
- [ ] A specific question a test can answer (for example "Can a case-study
      post reach founders in one category at an acceptable cost per qualified
      conversation?").
- [ ] Tracking on the studio site is in place and privacy-compliant.
- [ ] Capacity to handle the leads it creates.

### Test framework

1. **Hypothesis:** audience, message, proof asset, expected signal.
2. **Design:** one platform, one audience, one or two creative variants
   built from existing proof (a case study or essay — never a discount or
   urgency message).
3. **Cap and duration:** a fixed budget cap and end date set by the founder
   in the vault's `budgets.yaml`.
4. **Success and stop rules, set before launch:** primary measure is cost per
   qualified conversation (not clicks or likes); stop early on brand-safety
   issues or irrelevant enquiries.
5. **Readback:** compare against the same period's organic and referral
   results; decide scale, change or stop; record in the monthly review.

Promotion is honest: no inflated claims, no fake scarcity, no "award-winning"
unless true and current.

## Outputs

A test plan and readback (vault, because they contain spend); anonymised
learning in the monthly review.

## Quality gate

- [ ] All readiness conditions met.
- [ ] Hypothesis, cap, duration and stop rules written before launch.
- [ ] Measured on qualified conversations, not vanity metrics.
- [ ] Spend recorded only in the vault.

## Metrics

Spend, qualified conversations and cost per qualified conversation (vault);
see [growth metrics](metrics.md).

## Failure modes

- Spending to fill a thin pipeline instead of fixing proof or qualification.
- Optimising for clicks.
- Running tests without a stop rule.
