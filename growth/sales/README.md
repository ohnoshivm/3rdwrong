# Sales system

**Proposed.** How a contact becomes a qualified, well-understood opportunity
ready for a proposal: lifecycle stages L0 Lead, L1 Qualify and L2 Discover &
scope, plus the pipeline that tracks them and the review that learns from
every outcome.

## Principles

- **Diagnose before prescribing.** The studio earns the right to propose by
  understanding the problem better than anyone else the buyer speaks to.
- **Qualify honestly in both directions.** A clear "not yet" or "not us" is a
  good outcome. Refer work the studio should not take.
- **No pressure tactics.** No invented deadlines, artificial scarcity, or
  discounts for signing quickly. Real constraints (studio capacity, a launch
  date the client named) may be stated plainly.
- **Names and money stay in the vault.** Public material uses codenames and
  anonymised counts only ([D-0021](../../studio/decisions/0021-client-work-public-anonymised.md)).
