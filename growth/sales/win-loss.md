# Win/loss review

**Proposed.** A short, honest review of every decided opportunity, so the
studio learns why it wins and loses rather than guessing.

## Purpose

Turn each outcome into evidence about the ICP, the offers, the proposal and
the studio's proof — and feed that evidence into the
[learning loop](../../methods/learning-loop.md).

## When it applies

Within five working days of any decision: won, lost, or closed after "not
yet". Also when the studio declines an opportunity after discovery.

## Roles

| Role | Department | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reviewer | Sales — pipeline-forecasting | Writes the review from notes and the buyer's feedback |
| **Founder** | Sign-off | Reads each review; owns the monthly themes |

## Inputs

Pipeline record, qualification score, discovery notes, proposal (Business
engine), and — where the buyer agrees — a 10-minute feedback conversation.

## Steps

1. **Ask for feedback** with one short, no-pressure message: "Would you share
   in ten minutes what made the decision? It helps us improve." Accept a no.
2. **Render the `win-loss` bundle** into the vault and fill it: outcome,
   stage reached, source, offer, score at L1, the buyer's stated reasons in
   their words, the studio's interpretation (labelled), competitor or
   alternative chosen, price as a factor (yes/no/unknown; amounts stay in the
   vault), and one change to make.
3. **Separate evidence from interpretation.** Quote what was said; label
   guesses as **Interpretation**.
4. **Route the change** to its owner: ICP or qualification (Sales), offer or
   proposal (Business), proof gaps (Marketing), craft (Design, Engineering).
5. **Monthly:** read the month's reviews together; record themes and counts
   (anonymised) in the business review.

## Outputs

One review per decided opportunity in `{vault}/growth/win-loss/`; anonymised
monthly themes, for example "3 of 5 losses cited timing", with the sample size.

## Quality gate

- [ ] Filed within five working days of the decision.
- [ ] Buyer's words quoted where available; interpretation labelled.
- [ ] One concrete change with an owner, or "no change" with a reason.
- [ ] Themes published only as anonymised counts with sample size and date.

## Metrics

Win rate; loss reasons by category; share of decided opportunities with a
review filed. See [growth metrics](../metrics.md).

## Failure modes

- Reviewing only losses; wins teach as much.
- Blaming price by default without the buyer saying so.
- Publishing a single loss as a pattern.
