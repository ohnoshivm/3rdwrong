# Testimonials

**Proposed.** How the studio asks for, records, stores and uses client words —
only with written permission.

## Purpose

Let buyers hear from people the studio has worked with, without misquoting,
over-polishing or publishing anything the client has not approved.

## When it applies

At L9 close-out, and whenever a client volunteers praise that the studio may
want to use.

## Roles

| Role | Department | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Requester | Production — client-experience, or the founder | Asks at close-out |
| Keeper | Marketing — pr-awards | Stores testimonials and permissions in the vault |
| **Founder** | Sign-off | Approves each public use |

## Inputs

Close-out feedback; the client's own words; the contract's publication clause.

## Steps

1. **Ask** at the close-out conversation, once and plainly: "Would you be
   willing to say a few words about working with us that we could share?"
   Offer two or three prompts (what was the situation, what was it like
   working together, what changed). Accept a no without follow-up pressure.
2. **Record their words exactly** in the vault (`growth/testimonials/<codename>.md`).
   Light edits for length only, and only with their approval of the edited
   text.
3. **Get written permission** for the exact text and its attribution level:
   anonymous ("Founder, premium skincare brand"), role and company, or full
   name — and where it may appear (site, proposals, social). Store the
   permission with the text.
4. **Keep it in the vault until cleared.** A testimonial enters the
   repository only as an anonymised attribution with permission recorded, and
   never with personal data the repository prohibits.
5. **Review yearly** or when the client asks; remove on request without debate.

## Outputs

Testimonials and permissions in the vault; approved uses on the studio site
and in proposals.

## Quality gate

- [ ] Exact words, approved by the client.
- [ ] Written permission covering text, attribution level and places of use.
- [ ] No invented, composite or AI-generated testimonials, ever.
- [ ] Removal requests honoured promptly.

## Metrics

Testimonials requested and received per closed project.

## Failure modes

- Paraphrasing praise into a quote the client never said.
- Using a testimonial beyond its permitted places.
- Asking before the work is accepted.
