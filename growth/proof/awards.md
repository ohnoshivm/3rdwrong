# Awards strategy

**Proposed.** Which awards fit the studio's work, what each judges, how to
decide whether to enter, and how to submit well. Award fees and budgets are
decided and recorded in the vault.

## Purpose

Use awards to earn peer credibility and discovery for the digital-experience
pillar and, selectively, for identity work — without letting award taste
replace the client's brief or the studio's own direction.

## When it applies

After L7 launch of any digital-experience project, at L9 for identity
projects, and for strong self-initiated work (labelled as such where the
platform asks).

## Roles

| Role | Department | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Awards lead | Marketing — pr-awards | Shortlist, calendar, submissions, results log |
| Technical lead | Engineering | Performance, accessibility and build notes for digital entries |
| Clearance | Production — handover-retention | Client permission to enter work |
| **Founder** | Sign-off | Enter or skip; fee spend (vault) |

## Inputs

The live work, the case study, client permission, the
[taste references](../../taste/README.md) and the award teardowns in
[Award Site Teardowns](../../taste/award-site-teardowns.md).

## Steps

1. **Fit check.** Is the work strong for this award's criteria, and does
   recognition there reach people the studio wants to reach? If either
   answer is no, skip.
2. **Permission.** Client work needs the client's agreement to be entered and
   credited; record it with the clearance (D-0021).
3. **Cost decision.** Record the fee, the expected value (peer credibility,
   discovery, team morale) and the decision in the vault's `budgets.yaml`.
   The annual awards budget is a **founder decision**.
4. **Prepare** using the submission checklist.
5. **Submit**, log the entry and date, and plan distribution if it wins.
6. **Log results** and learning, whether or not it wins.

### Platforms and what they judge

| Award | What it judges (source) | Fit for the studio |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/about-evaluation/) | **Documented:** jury scores Design (40%), Usability (30%), Creativity (20%) and Content (10%); scores of 6.5 or more earn an Honorable Mention; the highest scores win Site of the Day; SOTD winners are reviewed by a developer jury for a Developer Award; monthly and yearly awards follow (Awwwards evaluation page, read 2026-10-23) | Strong for digital-experience launches and the studio site; reaches peers and digital buyers |
| [CSS Design Awards](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/about) | **Documented:** a judging panel scores sites for Website of the Day; sites averaging above 6 receive Special Kudos; public awards for UI, UX and Innovation combine public votes with judge scores. A detailed scoring rubric is not published on the page read 2026-10-23 | Good for digital work; another credible peer signal |
| [The FWA](https://thefwa.com/) | **Unknown:** criteria could not be verified from readable official content (see [discovery sources](../../taste/discovery-sources.md#the-fwa)). Check the official site before entering | Possible fit for experimental, interactive or 3D work |
| Identity and design awards (for example D&AD, Type Directors Club, ADC, and India's Kyoorius Creative Awards) | **Unknown here:** categories, criteria, deadlines and fees not reviewed for this page; verify on each official site | Candidates for identity-system work once there is a strong, cleared project; choose one or two, not all |

Awards reflect a jury's lens, not commercial effectiveness, accessibility or
client satisfaction. Never present an award as proof that work performed.

### Submission checklist

- [ ] Client permission recorded (or work labelled self-initiated).
- [ ] Live URL stable; no staging links; works on the jury's likely devices.
- [ ] Performance and accessibility checked by Engineering; known limits noted.
- [ ] Cover image and screenshots at each platform's current specifications.
- [ ] Short description: the idea and the decision, not adjectives.
- [ ] Credits: studio, collaborators and client as permitted; technologies.
- [ ] Category chosen deliberately.
- [ ] Fee decision recorded in the vault.
- [ ] Results and learning logged after judging.

### Calendar

- **Rolling daily awards** (Awwwards, CSS Design Awards): submit within a few
  weeks of launch, while the work is current and stable.
- **Annual awards:** keep a calendar in the vault with each award's entry
  window and deadline, checked on the official site each year; review it in
  the monthly growth review and decide entries at least a month before each
  deadline.

## Outputs

Entries, a results log and an awards calendar (vault); award badges and
mentions on the studio site and case studies only after results are
published.

## Quality gate

- [ ] Fit check and permission done before spending.
- [ ] Submission checklist complete.
- [ ] No award claim before the official result.
- [ ] Results logged with the platform's own wording.

## Metrics

Entries, results, enquiries attributed to award platforms (`inbound-award`),
and spend per entry (vault). See [growth metrics](../metrics.md).

## Failure modes

- Designing for juries instead of the client's audience.
- Entering everything; fees add up and weak entries signal weak judgement.
- Entering client work without permission.
- Overstating recognition ("award-winning studio" from one Honorable Mention).
