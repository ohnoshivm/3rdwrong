# Proof engine

**Proposed.** How finished work becomes credible, permitted proof: case
studies, testimonials, awards, and a portfolio and studio site that stay
current. Serves the growth part of lifecycle stage **L9 Close-out & case study**.

## Purpose

A new studio is judged on its proof. This engine makes sure every piece of
proof is true, permitted and well told — and that none is published early.

## When it applies

At L9 for every client project; whenever self-initiated or fictional
portfolio work is finished ([portfolio strategy](../../studio/portfolio-strategy.md));
and on the monthly portfolio review.

## Roles

| Role | Department | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proof lead | Marketing — pr-awards | Runs the case-study pipeline, awards and the update loop |
| Production | Media; Design and Engineering | Visuals, film, captures, technical write-ups |
| Clearance | Production — handover-retention | Confirms the contract's publication clause and permissions |
| **Founder** | Sign-off | Approves every publication, testimonial use and award entry |

## Inputs

The project's contract clearance status ([D-0021](../../studio/decisions/0021-client-work-public-anonymised.md)), the retrospective, project
files (vault), the client's feedback and the studio's publication rules.

## Steps

1. At L9, run the [case-study pipeline](case-studies.md): clearance check →
   interview → story → visuals → publish → distribute.
2. Ask for a testimonial and a referral in the same close-out conversation
   ([testimonials](testimonials.md), [referrals](../partnerships-and-referrals.md)).
3. For digital work, decide on an award entry ([awards](awards.md)).
4. Run the portfolio and studio-site update loop below every month.

### Portfolio and studio-site update loop (monthly, 30 minutes)

1. **Inventory.** List published, ready-to-publish and blocked proof
   (blocked by clearance, assets or time).
2. **Order.** The portfolio leads with the work closest to the positioning:
   identity systems first, then digital experience; label self-initiated and
   fictional work on every page.
3. **Retire.** Remove or demote work that no longer represents the studio's
   standard or direction; fewer, stronger pieces beat a full grid.
4. **Refresh.** Update the studio site, Behance and award profiles together so
   they do not contradict each other.
5. **Check.** Links work; pages pass the studio's own accessibility and
   performance checks; captions and alt text present.
6. **Record** what changed in the monthly content log.

## Outputs

Published case studies (`projects/<codename>/case-study.md` for cleared
projects); testimonials stored in the vault until cleared; award entries; an
up-to-date portfolio.

## Quality gate

- [ ] Every public piece of client proof has a recorded clearance.
- [ ] Self-initiated and fictional work labelled everywhere it appears.
- [ ] No outcome stated without evidence; hypotheses labelled.
- [ ] Studio site, Behance and award profiles consistent.

## Metrics

Case studies published per cleared project; time from L7 acceptance to case
study; testimonials collected; award entries and results; portfolio views to
enquiries ([growth metrics](../metrics.md)).

## Failure modes

- Publishing before clearance or before the client has launched.
- Polished images without the decisions behind them.
- Letting the portfolio go stale while client work is busy.
- Overstating results or implying awards prove effectiveness.
